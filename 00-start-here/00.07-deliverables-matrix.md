# Purpose

This document defines every deliverable produced during the Enterprise HIPAA Cybersecurity Assessment.

The Deliverables Matrix serves as the master inventory of assessment outputs. It establishes what artifacts are expected, when they are created, who owns them, and how they support executive decision-making.

The matrix also helps ensure every assessment engagement produces consistent, repeatable results regardless of the assessor performing the work.

---

# Objectives

After reviewing this document, the assessor will understand:

- Every deliverable produced during the engagement
- When each deliverable is created
- The purpose of each deliverable
- The intended audience for each deliverable

# Deliverable Categories

The assessment produces deliverables in five major categories.

| Category | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Planning | Define and organize the engagement |
| Assessment | Document assessment activities and results |
| Risk | Capture and prioritize enterprise risks |
| Remediation | Develop corrective actions and roadmaps |
| Executive Reporting | Communicate results to leadership |

# Deliverable Flow

The assessment produces deliverables in the following sequence.

```text
Client Engagement

↓

Assessment Planning

↓

Evidence Collection

↓

Assessment Results

↓

Findings

↓

Risk Register

↓

Recommendations

↓

Corrective Action Plan

↓

POA&M

↓

Executive Dashboard

↓

Board Report

↓

Final Assessment Report
```

Each deliverable builds upon information created during earlier phases.

- Accurate
- Complete
- Evidence-based
- Traceable
- Version controlled
- Peer reviewed
- Executive ready
