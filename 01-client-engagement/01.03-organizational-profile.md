# Ownership Model

The assessment distinguishes between business ownership, technical ownership, and control ownership.

| Ownership Type | Description | Example |
|----------------|-------------|---------|
| Business Owner | Responsible for business outcomes | Chief Nursing Officer |
| Technical Owner | Operates or supports the technology | IT Infrastructure Manager |
| Control Owner | Accountable for implementing and maintaining the control | Identity & Access Management Manager |

Understanding these distinctions helps ensure findings and recommendations are directed to the appropriate stakeholders.

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Verify reporting relationships during executive interviews.
- Confirm committee meeting frequency and documented decisions.
- Identify both business and technical owners for critical systems.

## Common Pitfalls

- Assuming technical owners are also control owners.
- Overlooking informal governance structures.
- Failing to document executive reporting lines.

## Red Flags

- No designated CISO or security leadership.
- Undefined governance committees.
- Unclear accountability for cybersecurity controls.
- Business owners unaware of their security responsibilities.
