# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Interview both business and technical stakeholders.
- Validate ownership rather than assuming job titles reflect responsibilities.
- Maintain the stakeholder register throughout the engagement.

## Common Pitfalls

- Assigning one person too many responsibilities.
- Failing to identify backup contacts.
- Not distinguishing between technical and control ownership.

## Red Flags

- No executive sponsor.
- Critical systems without an assigned owner.
- Multiple stakeholders claiming ownership of the same control.
- Stakeholders unavailable for interviews.
