# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Obtain formal approval before beginning assessment planning.
- Record any conditions or limitations placed on the approval.
- Ensure all major stakeholders understand their responsibilities before fieldwork begins.
- Archive the signed approval with the engagement documentation.

## Common Pitfalls

- Beginning assessment activities before formal approval.
- Treating approval as a formality instead of a governance checkpoint.
- Failing to document approval conditions.
- Not updating approvals after significant scope changes.

## Red Flags

- Executive sponsor refuses to approve the scope.
- Key stakeholders are not committed to participation.
- Major assumptions remain unresolved.
- Significant engagement risks remain unmitigated.
