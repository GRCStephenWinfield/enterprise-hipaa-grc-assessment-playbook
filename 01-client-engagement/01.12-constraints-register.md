# Purpose

This Constraints Register documents the known limitations that may affect the planning, execution, quality, scope, schedule, or outcomes of the Enterprise HIPAA Cybersecurity Assessment.

Unlike assumptions, which represent conditions believed to be true, constraints represent known restrictions that must be considered throughout the engagement.

Managing constraints enables realistic planning, informed decision-making, and transparent communication regarding factors that may influence assessment results.

# Purpose of the Register

This register identifies constraints related to:

- Schedule
- Budget
- Resources
- Technology
- Regulatory obligations
- Clinical operations
- Third-party dependencies
- Evidence availability

Each constraint should be evaluated to determine its impact on assessment execution and reporting.

# Constraint Management Process

For each identified constraint:

1. Document the constraint.
2. Assign an owner.
3. Assess business impact.
4. Define a mitigation strategy.
5. Monitor throughout the engagement.
6. Escalate if the constraint materially affects scope, schedule, or quality.

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Distinguish clearly between assumptions and constraints.
- Reassess constraints at major project milestones.
- Document any new constraints through formal change control.
- Escalate constraints that threaten project success.

## Common Pitfalls

- Treating constraints as temporary inconveniences rather than planning realities.
- Failing to assign ownership.
- Ignoring the cumulative effect of multiple medium-impact constraints.

## Red Flags

- Constraints are identified but not monitored.
- Clinical priorities repeatedly delay assessment activities.
- Evidence cannot be obtained due to unresolved access restrictions.
- Scope or quality is compromised without executive awareness.
