# RACI Definitions

| Letter | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **R** | Responsible – Performs the work |
| **A** | Accountable – Ultimately answerable for the outcome |
| **C** | Consulted – Provides input before decisions are made |
| **I** | Informed – Kept aware of progress or decisions |

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Ensure every activity has one and only one **Accountable** role.
- Review the RACI during the kickoff meeting.
- Update the matrix when organizational changes occur.
- Use the RACI to resolve ownership disputes.

## Common Pitfalls

- Assigning multiple Accountable roles.
- Confusing Responsible with Accountable.
- Failing to communicate changes to the RACI.
- Leaving activities without an assigned owner.

## Red Flags

- No clearly accountable executive.
- Conflicting ownership for critical controls.
- Stakeholders unaware of their responsibilities.
- Activities with no Responsible party.
