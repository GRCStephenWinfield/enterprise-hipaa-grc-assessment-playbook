# Purpose

This Assumptions Register documents the planning assumptions upon which the Enterprise HIPAA Cybersecurity Assessment is based.

Assumptions represent conditions believed to be true for planning purposes but which have not yet been fully validated. During the engagement, assumptions should be confirmed or updated as evidence is collected.

Managing assumptions reduces uncertainty, improves planning accuracy, and helps identify emerging risks if assumptions prove to be incorrect.

# Assumption Validation Process

Each assumption will be assigned one of the following statuses:

| Status | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Pending | Not yet validated |
| Confirmed | Verified through interviews or evidence |
| Updated | Modified based on new information |
| Invalid | Proven incorrect and requires reassessment |

If an assumption becomes invalid, the assessment team will evaluate the impact on scope, schedule, risks, and deliverables.

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Challenge assumptions early in the engagement.
- Validate assumptions with objective evidence whenever possible.
- Review assumptions during major project milestones.
- Update assumptions when organizational conditions change.

## Common Pitfalls

- Treating assumptions as facts.
- Failing to revisit assumptions after planning.
- Omitting ownership for critical assumptions.

## Red Flags

- Numerous assumptions remain unvalidated late in the engagement.
- Stakeholders disagree on fundamental planning assumptions.
- Critical planning decisions rely on unsupported assumptions.
- Invalid assumptions are not reflected in updated plans.
