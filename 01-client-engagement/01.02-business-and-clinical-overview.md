# Executive Overview

Magnolia Regional Medical Center (MRMC) is a regional healthcare system dedicated to delivering high-quality, patient-centered medical care across southern Louisiana.

The organization operates a full-service acute care hospital supported by outpatient clinics, diagnostic centers, specialty practices, emergency services, and telehealth capabilities.

Clinical operations depend heavily on secure, reliable information systems to ensure patient safety, regulatory compliance, and uninterrupted healthcare delivery.

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Understand how patient care is delivered before assessing technology.
- Identify business-critical workflows early in the engagement.
- Validate assumptions with operational leaders.

## Common Pitfalls

- Treating administrative systems as less critical than clinical systems.
- Overlooking dependencies between departments.
- Failing to distinguish mission-critical processes from supporting services.

## Red Flags

- Undefined business priorities.
- Poor documentation of critical workflows.
- Limited executive visibility into cybersecurity.
- Unclear ownership of key business processes.
