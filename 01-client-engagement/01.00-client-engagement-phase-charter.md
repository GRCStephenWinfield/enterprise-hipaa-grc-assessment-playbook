# Purpose

The Client Engagement Phase Charter formally authorizes the beginning of the client engagement activities and establishes the objectives, governance, scope, responsibilities, success criteria, and expected outcomes for this phase.

This charter serves as the governing document for all work performed during the Client Engagement phase.

---

# Phase Mission

Develop a comprehensive understanding of Magnolia Regional Medical Center, its business operations, regulatory obligations, organizational structure, and assessment objectives before beginning assessment planning.

The quality of this phase directly impacts the success of every subsequent phase.

---

# Business Objectives

The assessment team will:

- Understand the organization
- Understand clinical operations
- Understand business operations
- Identify critical assets
- Identify key stakeholders
- Define assessment boundaries
- Establish communication channels
- Obtain executive sponsorship

# Success Criteria

The Client Engagement phase is considered successful when:

- Executive sponsor approves the engagement.
- Assessment scope is finalized.
- Organizational stakeholders are identified.
- Assessment boundaries are documented.
- Regulatory obligations are confirmed.
- Communication expectations are established.
- The assessment team is authorized to proceed.

---

# Risks

Common risks during this phase include:

⚠️ Undefined assessment scope

⚠️ Missing executive sponsorship

⚠️ Unavailable stakeholders

⚠️ Incomplete organizational documentation

⚠️ Unclear regulatory requirements

---

# Dependencies

This phase depends on:

- Completion of Phase 1 – Orientation

# Phase Exit Criteria

The phase is complete when:

- All required documents have been completed.
- Executive approval has been obtained.
- Assessment scope has been approved.
- Stakeholders have accepted their responsibilities.
- The assessment team is authorized to begin Assessment Planning.

---

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Meet with executive leadership before interviewing operational staff.
- Confirm assessment objectives in writing.
- Establish communication expectations early.
- Validate organizational charts before identifying stakeholders.
- Resolve scope disagreements before planning begins.

## Common Pitfalls

- Scope expands without formal approval.
- Stakeholders are identified too late.
- Business objectives are assumed instead of confirmed.
- Clinical operations are not adequately represented.

## Red Flags

- No executive sponsor assigned.
- Undefined assessment objectives.
- Incomplete organizational documentation.
- Disagreement regarding assessment scope.
- Missing regulatory ownership.
