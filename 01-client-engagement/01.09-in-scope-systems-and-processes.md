# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Validate inventories against multiple sources (CMDB, network diagrams, cloud inventories, and interviews).
- Confirm ownership with department leaders rather than relying solely on documentation.
- Identify shared infrastructure that supports multiple business services.

## Common Pitfalls

- Assuming the CMDB is complete.
- Overlooking SaaS applications.
- Failing to classify systems by business criticality.
- Not identifying dependencies between systems.

## Red Flags

- No authoritative asset inventory.
- Unknown system owners.
- Tier 0 systems without documented recovery objectives.
- Conflicting inventories from different departments.
- Critical applications not covered by backup or monitoring.
