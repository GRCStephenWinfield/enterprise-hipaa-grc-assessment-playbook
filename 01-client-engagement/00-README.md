# 01 – Client Engagement

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The Client Engagement phase establishes the business context, governance structure, project objectives, scope, stakeholders, and executive authorization for the cybersecurity assessment.

This documentation represents the first phase of a professional consulting engagement and provides the information necessary to understand the organization before technical assessment activities begin.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Client Engagement phase is to establish a common understanding between the client and the assessment team by defining:

- Organizational background
- Business and clinical objectives
- Regulatory obligations
- Assessment scope
- Project governance
- Stakeholder responsibilities
- Risk assumptions
- Executive approval

The deliverables in this section form the foundation for all subsequent assessment activities.

- Planning assumptions
