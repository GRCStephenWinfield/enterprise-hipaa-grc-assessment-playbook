# Purpose

The Client Engagement phase establishes the foundation for the cybersecurity assessment.

Before any assessment activities begin, the assessment team must understand the organization, define the scope of work, identify stakeholders, establish governance, and obtain agreement regarding objectives, timelines, and expected deliverables.

A successful engagement begins with a shared understanding between the assessment team and the client.

---

# Phase Objectives

Upon completion of this phase, the assessment team will have:

- Established the assessment scope
- Identified key stakeholders
- Documented organizational objectives
- Identified regulatory obligations
- Defined in-scope systems
- Defined out-of-scope systems
- Established communication channels
- Obtained engagement approval
