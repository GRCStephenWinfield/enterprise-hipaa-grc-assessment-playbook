Exclusions are documented to:

# Out-of-Scope Activities

The following activities are excluded unless separately authorized:

- Penetration testing
- Red-team exercises
- Social engineering campaigns
- Wireless security testing
- Physical penetration testing
- Destructive testing
- Malware simulations
- Load or stress testing
- Configuration changes to production systems
- Live exploitation of vulnerabilities

---

# Third-Party Exclusions

The following are outside the assessment scope unless specifically approved:

- Vendor internal security programs
- Cloud provider internal controls
- External payment processor infrastructure
- Business associate internal environments
- Managed service provider operational environments

The assessment will evaluate governance over these providers, not their internal security operations.

---

# Geographic Exclusions

Assessment activities do not include:

- Remote vendor facilities
- Off-site archival storage
- Third-party data centers without client authorization
- International affiliate locations (if applicable)

---

# Regulatory Exclusions

This engagement does **not** constitute:

- A formal HIPAA compliance certification
- A PCI DSS Qualified Security Assessor (QSA) assessment
- A HITRUST certification assessment
- A SOC examination
- A financial audit
- A legal opinion regarding regulatory compliance

---

# Residual Risk Considerations

Excluding systems or activities from the assessment does **not** eliminate associated organizational risk.

Residual risks resulting from exclusions should be:

- Documented
- Communicated to executive leadership
- Considered during enterprise risk management
- Reassessed if scope changes occur

---

# Change Control

If an excluded item must be included during the engagement:

1. Submit a formal change request.
2. Assess schedule and resource impacts.
3. Evaluate risks.
4. Obtain executive approval.
5. Update the Assessment Scope and Statement of Work.

No excluded item shall be added without approved change control.

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Document exclusions explicitly rather than assuming they are understood.
- Explain why each item is excluded.
- Discuss residual risks with executive leadership.

## Common Pitfalls

- Excluding critical systems without documenting the impact.
- Allowing verbal scope changes.
- Assuming vendor-managed systems do not require governance review.

## Red Flags

- Client requests significant additions after fieldwork begins.
- Critical business processes are excluded without justification.
- Stakeholders have conflicting expectations regarding scope.
- No formal change-control process exists.
