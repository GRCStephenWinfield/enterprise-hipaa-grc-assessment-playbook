# Purpose

This Engagement Risk Register identifies, evaluates, and tracks risks that may affect the successful planning, execution, quality, schedule, or delivery of the Enterprise HIPAA Cybersecurity Assessment.

Unlike the Enterprise Risk Register created later in the assessment, this register focuses exclusively on risks to the engagement itself.

# Risk Monitoring Process

The Engagement Risk Register will be reviewed:

- Weekly during the engagement
- Before each major project milestone
- After any significant scope change
- Following any major project issue
- Prior to executive status updates

---

# Risk Escalation Criteria

Immediate escalation is required when:

- A Critical engagement risk is identified.
- A High risk becomes imminent.
- Scope, schedule, or quality objectives are materially threatened.
- Executive sponsorship is affected.
- The engagement can no longer meet agreed deliverables without formal change.

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Review engagement risks during every weekly status meeting.
- Update risk ratings as conditions change.
- Escalate significant risks early.
- Distinguish project risks from client cybersecurity risks.

## Common Pitfalls

- Ignoring engagement risks while focusing only on technical risks.
- Failing to assign risk owners.
- Delaying escalation until deadlines are missed.

## Red Flags

- Repeated delays in evidence collection.
- Stakeholder participation declines.
- Frequent requests for undocumented scope changes.
- Executive sponsorship weakens during the engagement.
