# Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery Workpaper Library

> **Phase 11 – Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery**

This folder contains the detailed technical workpapers supporting the Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery assessment.

Each workpaper documents the control objective, framework mapping, evidence requirements, testing procedures, observations, findings, maturity assessment, and conclusions for a specific BCDR capability.

# Standard Workpaper Contents

Every workpaper includes:

- Executive Summary
- Business Objective
- Control Objective
- Framework Mapping
- Evidence Requirements
- Testing Procedures
- Sample Validation
- Risk Assessment
- Auditor Observations
- Findings
- Recommendations
- Maturity Assessment
- Traceability
- Conclusion
- Quality Assurance Checklist
