# 11 – Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery (BCDR) domain evaluates the organization's ability to prepare for, respond to, recover from, and continuously improve resilience against operational disruptions, cyber incidents, natural disasters, and technology failures.

This assessment examines governance, business impact analysis, recovery objectives, continuity planning, disaster recovery procedures, backup and restoration, crisis communications, technical failover, cloud resilience, and continuous improvement.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to determine whether the organization can maintain critical business operations, protect patient care, recover technology services, and restore normal operations within acceptable recovery objectives.

The assessment evaluates:

- Business Continuity Governance
- Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
- Critical Business Processes
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTO)
- Recovery Point Objectives (RPO)
- Business Continuity Plans
- Clinical Downtime Procedures
- Disaster Recovery Plans
- Backup Strategy
- Backup Validation
- Alternate Processing
- Crisis Management
- Tabletop Exercises
- Technical Recovery Testing
- Third-Party & Cloud Continuity
- Continuous Improvement
