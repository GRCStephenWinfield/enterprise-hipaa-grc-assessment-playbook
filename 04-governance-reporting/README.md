# 04 – Governance Reporting

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The Governance Reporting phase consolidates the results of the Governance assessment into executive-level reporting artifacts. These documents communicate assessment results, identified risks, recommended corrective actions, and overall governance maturity to executive leadership and the Board.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Governance Reporting is to translate technical assessment results into actionable business information for leadership.

The reporting package includes:

- Governance Findings
- Enterprise Risks
- Management Recommendations
- Plan of Action & Milestones (POA&M)
- Executive Summary
- Executive Scorecard

These documents support executive decision-making, regulatory readiness, and continuous improvement initiatives.

# Deliverables Produced

At the completion of the Governance Reporting phase, executive leadership receives:

- Governance Findings Register
- Governance Risk Register
- Governance Recommendations Register
- Governance POA&M
- Executive Summary
- Executive Scorecard

These deliverables provide management with a comprehensive view of governance effectiveness and prioritize corrective actions based on organizational risk.

---

# Reviewer Guide

Recommended review sequence:

1. Governance Findings Register
2. Governance Risk Register
3. Governance Recommendations Register
4. Governance POA&M
5. Governance Executive Summary & Scorecard

This sequence follows the natural progression from identifying issues through planning remediation and communicating results to executive leadership.
