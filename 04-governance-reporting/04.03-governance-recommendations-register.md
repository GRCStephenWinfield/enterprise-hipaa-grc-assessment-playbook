# REC-GOV-001 — Standardize Business-Unit Cybersecurity Responsibilities

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Classification | Corrective Action / Process Improvement |
| Related Finding | FIN-GOV-001 |
| Related Risk | RSK-GOV-001 |
| Related Control | CTRL-GOV-004 |
| Related Workpaper | WP-GOV-004 |
| Proposed POA&M | POAM-GOV-001 |
| Priority | Moderate |
| Responsible Owner | Chief Information Officer |
| Supporting Owners | Human Resources Director, CISO, Business Unit Leaders |
| Target Completion | Q4 FY2027 |

## Recommendation

Magnolia Regional Medical Center should establish a standardized process for documenting, approving, communicating, and annually validating cybersecurity responsibilities assigned to business-unit system and process owners.

The process should include:

1. A standard cybersecurity-responsibility statement for business owners.
2. Updated job descriptions for system and process owners.
3. Defined responsibilities for risk ownership, evidence support, incident escalation, access approval, and remediation.
4. Primary and alternate owners for critical systems.
5. Annual ownership certification.
6. Human Resources and Information Security review.
7. Updates following organizational or technology changes.
8. Central maintenance of ownership records.

## Root Cause Addressed

Business-unit cybersecurity responsibilities evolved operationally without corresponding updates to formal role descriptions and governance records.

## Expected Benefits

- Clearer accountability
- Faster incident and risk escalation
- Improved audit readiness
- Better continuity during personnel transitions
- Stronger remediation ownership
- Reduced ownership disputes

## Success Measures

| Measure | Target |
|---------|-------:|
| Critical Systems with Documented Business Owners | 100% |
| Business Owners with Updated Responsibility Statements | 100% |
| Annual Ownership Certifications Completed | 100% |
| Systems Without Alternate Owners | 0 |
| Ownership Exceptions Older Than 30 Days | 0 |

## Proposed Management Response

### MR-GOV-001

Management agrees with the recommendation.

The CIO, CISO, and Human Resources Director will develop standardized cybersecurity responsibility language and update role records for all critical business-system owners.

Ownership certification will be incorporated into the annual governance review.

# REC-GOV-003 — Establish Time-Bound Risk Acceptance Governance

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Classification | Corrective Action / Risk Reduction |
| Related Finding | FIN-GOV-003 |
| Related Risk | RSK-GOV-003 |
| Related Control | CTRL-GOV-007 |
| Related Workpaper | WP-GOV-007 |
| Proposed POA&M | POAM-GOV-003 |
| Priority | Immediate |
| Responsible Owner | Enterprise Risk Manager |
| Supporting Owners | CISO, Risk Owners, Governance Committee |
| Target Completion | Q1 FY2027 |
| Executive Oversight | Required |

## Recommendation

Magnolia Regional Medical Center should implement a time-bound cybersecurity risk-acceptance process that requires expiration, reassessment, reauthorization, and automated escalation.

The revised process should:

1. Require expiration dates for every risk acceptance.
2. Prohibit indefinite risk acceptance.
3. Establish maximum acceptance periods based on residual risk.
4. Require periodic residual-risk reassessment.
5. Require validation of compensating controls.
6. Require formal reauthorization before extension.
7. Automatically notify owners before expiration.
8. Escalate overdue acceptances.
9. Report aging to executive leadership.
10. Require closure validation when the risk is remediated.

## Recommended Maximum Acceptance Periods

| Residual Risk | Maximum Initial Period | Review Frequency |
|---------------|-----------------------:|-----------------|
| Critical | 90 Days | Monthly |
| High | 180 Days | Quarterly |
| Moderate | 12 Months | Semiannual |
| Low | 24 Months | Annual |

## Immediate Corrective Actions

Within 60 days:

- Review all existing risk acceptances.
- Assign expiration dates.
- Reassess residual risk.
- Validate compensating controls.
- Escalate expired High and Critical acceptances.
- Obtain reauthorization where necessary.

## Root Cause Addressed

The current risk-acceptance standard defines approval authority but does not consistently establish expiration, reassessment, or reauthorization requirements.

## Expected Benefits

- Reduced long-term exposure
- Stronger executive oversight
- Improved risk accountability
- Better control over remediation deferrals
- Improved HIPAA risk-management defensibility
- Elimination of indefinite risk acceptance

## Success Measures

| Measure | Target |
|---------|-------:|
| Risk Acceptances with Expiration Dates | 100% |
| High and Critical Acceptances Reviewed on Time | 100% |
| Expired Acceptances Without Reauthorization | 0 |
| Compensating Controls Validated | 100% |
| Accepted Risks Older Than Maximum Period | 0 |
| Risk-Acceptance Aging Reported to Executives | Quarterly |

## Proposed Management Response

### MR-GOV-003

Management agrees with the finding and recommendation.

The Enterprise Risk Manager and CISO will revise the Risk Acceptance Standard, review all current acceptances, and implement automated review and escalation workflows.

Existing High and Critical acceptances will receive priority reassessment.

# Recommendation Closure Requirements

- The required action is implemented.
- Supporting evidence is available.
- Associated POA&M milestones are complete.
- The affected control is retested where applicable.
- The related finding is validated as remediated.
- Residual risk is recalculated.
- The responsible owner approves closure.
- Quality review is complete.
- Executive approval is obtained where required.
