# Priority Findings

## FIN-GOV-003 — Risk Acceptances Are Not Consistently Time-Bound or Periodically Reauthorized

**Severity:** High  
**Residual Risk:** High  
**Owner:** Enterprise Risk Manager  
**POA&M:** POAM-GOV-003

Selected risk acceptances lacked expiration dates or documented periodic reauthorization.

This creates the possibility that accepted risks will remain active after:

- Threat conditions change
- Systems change
- Compensating controls weaken
- Business justification expires
- Risk exceeds approved tolerance

### Required Executive Action

- Review all active risk acceptances.
- Assign expiration dates.
- Reassess High and Critical accepted risks.
- Validate compensating controls.
- Implement automated reminders and escalation.
- Prohibit indefinite High or Critical risk acceptance.
