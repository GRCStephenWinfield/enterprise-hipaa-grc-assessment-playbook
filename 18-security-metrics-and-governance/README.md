# 18 – Security Metrics & Governance

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The Security Metrics & Governance (SMG) domain evaluates the organization's ability to establish cybersecurity governance, align security strategy with business objectives, measure performance, communicate cyber risk to leadership, and continuously improve the cybersecurity program.

This assessment examines governance structures, cybersecurity strategy, executive accountability, policy governance, risk appetite, security metrics, board reporting, budgeting, workforce governance, audit oversight, regulatory governance, steering committees, risk acceptance, security culture, and continuous improvement.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to determine whether executive leadership has implemented an effective cybersecurity governance program that supports strategic objectives, regulatory compliance, enterprise risk management, and informed executive decision-making.

The assessment evaluates:

- Cybersecurity Governance Structure
- Cybersecurity Strategy
- Business Alignment
- Roles & Responsibilities
- Policy Governance
- Risk Appetite & Tolerance
- Security Metrics
- Executive Reporting
- Board Reporting
- Budget Governance
- Workforce Governance
- Audit Oversight
- Regulatory Governance
- Steering Committees
- Risk Acceptance
- Security Culture
- Continuous Improvement
