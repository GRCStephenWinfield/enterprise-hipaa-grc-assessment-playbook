# 16 – Incident Response & Crisis Management

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The Incident Response & Crisis Management (IRCM) domain evaluates the organization's ability to prepare for, detect, respond to, contain, eradicate, recover from, and continuously improve its response to cybersecurity incidents and enterprise crises.

This assessment examines incident response governance, response planning, case management, investigations, containment, recovery, digital forensics, crisis communications, regulatory notifications, ransomware response, cloud and third-party incidents, readiness exercises, and continuous improvement.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to determine whether the organization maintains an effective Incident Response and Crisis Management capability that minimizes business disruption, protects critical assets, meets regulatory obligations, and continually improves operational resilience.

The assessment evaluates:

- Incident Response Governance
- Incident Response Plans & Playbooks
- Incident Intake & Case Management
- Incident Classification & Escalation
- Incident Investigation
- Containment
- Eradication
- Recovery & Business Validation
- Digital Forensics
- Crisis Communications
- Regulatory Notifications
- Ransomware Response
- Cloud Incidents
- Third-Party Incidents
- Insider Threat Incidents
- Medical Device Incidents
- Tabletop Exercises
- Lessons Learned
- Continuous Improvement
