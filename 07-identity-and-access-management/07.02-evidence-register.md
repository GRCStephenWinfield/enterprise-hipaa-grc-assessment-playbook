# Purpose

This Evidence Register identifies the documentation, technical evidence, reports, configurations, and supporting artifacts required to assess the Identity & Access Management (IAM) control environment.

The register provides traceability between assessment evidence and the applicable IAM workpapers.

---

# Evidence Collection Requirements

Evidence should:

- Be current and approved.
- Cover the defined assessment period.
- Be obtained from authoritative systems.
- Be complete and unaltered.
- Demonstrate operating effectiveness.
- Support testing conclusions.
