# Executive Recommendation

The Identity & Access Management program demonstrates a mature governance structure and provides a strong foundation for protecting enterprise and clinical identities. The most significant opportunities for improvement involve strengthening privileged access governance, automating identity lifecycle management, improving cloud identity controls, enhancing service account security, and expanding identity monitoring capabilities.

Completion of the approved Plan of Action & Milestones (POA&M) is expected to reduce residual IAM risk, improve operational efficiency through automation, and further align the organization with Zero Trust principles and industry best practices.
