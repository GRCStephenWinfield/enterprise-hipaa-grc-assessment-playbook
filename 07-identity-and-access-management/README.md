# 07 – Identity & Access Management

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The Identity & Access Management (IAM) domain evaluates the organization's ability to govern digital identities, authenticate users, authorize access to enterprise resources, and continuously monitor privileged and non-privileged access throughout the identity lifecycle.

This assessment examines identity governance, user provisioning, privileged access management, authentication controls, cloud identity, remote access, clinical system access, and continuous improvement to determine the maturity of the organization's IAM program.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to determine whether enterprise identities are properly governed, authenticated, authorized, monitored, and deprovisioned in accordance with security policies and regulatory requirements.

The assessment evaluates:

- Identity Governance
- User Lifecycle Management
- Multi-Factor Authentication
- Password Management
- Privileged Access Management
- Service Account Management
- Role-Based Access Control
- Access Reviews & Certifications
- Third-Party Access
- Remote Access Security
- Break-Glass Accounts
- Cloud Identity Management
- Clinical System Access
- Identity Monitoring
- Continuous IAM Improvement
