# Purpose

This Plan of Action & Milestones (POA&M) documents the corrective actions required to remediate findings identified during the Identity & Access Management assessment.

The POA&M establishes accountability, implementation milestones, target completion dates, and status tracking to support continuous risk reduction and executive oversight.

---

# POA&M Status Definitions

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Open | Remediation not yet started |
| In Progress | Corrective actions underway |
| Pending Validation | Awaiting verification of implementation |
| Completed | Remediation verified and closed |
| Risk Accepted | Executive management formally accepted the residual risk |

# Milestone Schedule

## Phase 1 — Immediate (0–30 Days)

- HR-to-IAM integration
- User deprovisioning automation
- Critical privileged account review
- Cloud administrator validation

---

## Phase 2 — Short Term (30–90 Days)

- Expand PAM deployment
- Improve service account governance
- Strengthen remote access controls
- Complete clinical access reviews
- Modernize authentication controls
- Automate access certifications

---

## Phase 3 — Strategic (3–12 Months)

- Expand Zero Trust identity architecture
- Mature ITDR capabilities
- Increase IAM automation
- Improve executive reporting
- Perform maturity benchmarking
- Optimize cloud identity governance

# Success Measures

The POA&M will be considered successfully executed when:

- 100% of Critical actions are completed.
- High-priority findings are remediated within established timelines.
- Quarterly executive progress reviews are completed.
- Residual risks are formally accepted or mitigated.
- IAM maturity score improves year over year.
- Audit findings related to IAM decrease.

---

# Executive Reporting

Progress should be reported:

- Monthly to the Information Security Steering Committee.
- Quarterly to Executive Leadership.
- Quarterly to Enterprise Risk Management.
- Quarterly to the Audit Committee.
- Annually to the Board of Directors.
