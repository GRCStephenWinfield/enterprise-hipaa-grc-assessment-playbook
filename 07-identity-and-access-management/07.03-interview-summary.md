# Purpose

This document identifies the personnel who will participate in the Identity & Access Management assessment and establishes standardized interview procedures.

Interview responses will be validated against technical evidence, configuration data, system reports, and documentation obtained during the assessment.

---

# Interview Objectives

The interviews are intended to:

- Validate IAM governance processes.
- Confirm control ownership.
- Understand identity lifecycle procedures.
- Verify authentication and authorization controls.
- Identify undocumented practices.
- Validate technical evidence.
- Assess operational effectiveness.

# Standard Interview Questions

Each interview should address:

1. Describe your responsibilities related to IAM.
2. What policies govern your activities?
3. How are access requests approved?
4. How are privileged accounts managed?
5. How are exceptions documented?
6. What reports do you review?
7. How do you identify control failures?
8. What metrics are reported to leadership?
9. What improvements are currently planned?
10. What challenges affect your area?

---

# Interview Validation Criteria

Interview responses should be:

- Supported by documentation.
- Supported by technical evidence.
- Consistent with policy.
- Consistent across interview participants.
- Reproducible through testing.

Any inconsistencies should be documented as observations or potential findings.
