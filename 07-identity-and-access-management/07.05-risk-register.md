# Purpose

This Risk Register documents the enterprise risks identified during the Identity & Access Management assessment.

Each risk has been evaluated using organizational risk management criteria and includes inherent risk, existing controls, residual risk, business impact, regulatory considerations, assigned ownership, and recommended treatment strategy.

# Highest Priority Risks

The following risks require executive attention:

1. Delayed user account deprovisioning
2. Standing privileged administrator access
3. Unmanaged service accounts
4. Excessive cloud administrative privileges
5. Clinical system access governance
6. Identity threat detection and monitoring

---

# Risk Themes

Common risk patterns observed include:

- Excessive privileged access
- Identity lifecycle management weaknesses
- Cloud identity governance maturity
- Manual access certification activities
- Service account governance
- Identity monitoring coverage

---

# Recommended Enterprise Risk Priorities

## Immediate (0–30 Days)

- Address Critical risks.
- Validate privileged administrator access.
- Review terminated user accounts.
- Inventory service accounts.

---

## Near Term (30–90 Days)

- Expand Just-In-Time privileged access.
- Improve cloud identity governance.
- Strengthen access certification automation.
- Reduce authentication exceptions.

---

## Strategic (3–12 Months)

- Expand identity automation.
- Mature ITDR capabilities.
- Increase Zero Trust adoption.
- Enhance IAM analytics and reporting.
- Continue IAM maturity benchmarking.
