# Purpose

The Identity & Access Management (IAM) assessment evaluates the effectiveness of Magnolia Regional Medical Center's controls governing digital identities and access to enterprise information systems.

The assessment determines whether identities are properly created, managed, authenticated, authorized, monitored, reviewed, and removed throughout the identity lifecycle while supporting the principles of least privilege, separation of duties, and zero trust.

The objective is to ensure that only authorized individuals, services, and devices have appropriate access to enterprise resources and electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI).

---

# Assessment Objectives

The assessment will determine whether the organization:

- Maintains effective identity governance.
- Enforces strong authentication.
- Implements multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Applies least privilege.
- Conducts periodic access reviews.
- Governs privileged accounts.
- Secures service accounts.
- Controls third-party access.
- Manages identity lifecycle activities.
- Monitors authentication events.
- Detects unauthorized access.
- Supports Zero Trust principles.

# Success Criteria

The IAM program will be considered effective if:

- Identity lifecycle processes are consistently followed.
- Access is based on business need.
- MFA is enforced where required.
- Privileged accounts are tightly controlled.
- Access reviews are completed on schedule.
- Identity monitoring detects unauthorized activity.
- IAM governance supports HIPAA and enterprise security objectives.
