# Purpose

This Findings Register summarizes all observations identified during the Identity & Access Management assessment.

Each finding includes the affected control area, risk rating, business impact, supporting evidence, and recommended management response.

The register serves as the primary source document for executive reporting, remediation planning, risk management, and audit follow-up activities.

# Root Cause Summary

The assessment identified several recurring themes:

- Manual IAM processes remain in selected business units.
- Access recertification activities require greater automation.
- Cloud identity governance requires further maturity.
- Legacy authentication dependencies continue to create operational exceptions.
- Identity monitoring capabilities continue to mature.

---

# Business Impact

The identified findings may increase the organization's exposure to:

- Unauthorized access to enterprise systems.
- Unauthorized disclosure of ePHI.
- Privilege escalation.
- Identity compromise.
- Insider threats.
- HIPAA compliance deficiencies.
- Operational disruption.
- Audit observations.

---

# Overall Assessment

Overall, Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a mature Identity & Access Management program with well-established governance, authentication, authorization, and monitoring controls.

The identified findings primarily relate to opportunities for greater automation, stronger privileged access governance, improved cloud identity management, enhanced access review processes, and continued adoption of Zero Trust identity controls.

No pervasive or systemic failures were identified; however, timely remediation of the Critical and High findings is recommended.
