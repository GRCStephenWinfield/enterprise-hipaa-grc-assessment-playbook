# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center performs periodic access reviews and certifications to verify that users retain only the access necessary to perform their assigned job responsibilities.

The assessment determines whether management performs timely access recertifications, validates privileged access, identifies inappropriate permissions, and remediates excessive or unauthorized access.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Performs scheduled user access reviews.
- Requires manager certification of user access.
- Reviews privileged access separately.
- Reviews clinical application access.
- Performs segregation of duties (SoD) reviews.
- Documents remediation activities.
- Maintains evidence of completed certifications.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to perform periodic access reviews may result in:

- Privilege creep.
- Unauthorized access.
- Orphaned accounts.
- Excessive permissions.
- Segregation of duties conflicts.
- Insider threats.
- Regulatory noncompliance.
- Audit findings.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Quarterly certifications completed.
- Managers actively review access.
- Privileged accounts separately reviewed.
- Timely remediation performed.
- Executive oversight established.

### Partially Effective

- Reviews performed.
- Some certifications delayed.
- Minor remediation backlog.
- Limited executive reporting.

### Ineffective

- Reviews inconsistent.
- Certifications incomplete.
- Excessive access retained.
- Remediation not tracked.
- Executive oversight absent.
