# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center has established a formal Identity Governance program that defines accountability, policies, standards, oversight, and strategic direction for Identity & Access Management (IAM).

The assessment determines whether governance supports secure identity lifecycle management, regulatory compliance, least privilege, Zero Trust principles, and enterprise risk management.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Maintains formal IAM governance.
- Assigns IAM ownership and accountability.
- Maintains approved IAM policies and standards.
- Defines identity governance roles and responsibilities.
- Reviews IAM performance regularly.
- Aligns IAM strategy with enterprise risk.
- Continuously improves IAM governance.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to maintain effective IAM governance may result in:

- Inconsistent identity management.
- Unauthorized access.
- Weak accountability.
- Excessive user privileges.
- Regulatory noncompliance.
- Inadequate executive oversight.
- Increased cybersecurity risk.
- Identity lifecycle failures.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Governance formally established.
- Executive oversight active.
- Policies current.
- Roles clearly assigned.
- Continuous improvement demonstrated.

### Partially Effective

- Governance exists.
- Minor documentation gaps.
- Limited executive engagement.
- Improvement activities inconsistent.

### Ineffective

- Governance undefined.
- Policies outdated.
- Executive oversight absent.
- Roles unclear.
- Continuous improvement not evident.
