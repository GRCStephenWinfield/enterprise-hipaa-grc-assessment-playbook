# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains an effective continuous improvement program for Identity & Access Management (IAM).

The assessment determines whether the organization measures IAM performance, conducts maturity assessments, tracks remediation activities, leverages automation, incorporates lessons learned, and continuously strengthens IAM capabilities through governance and strategic planning.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Measures IAM performance using KPIs and KRIs.
- Performs periodic IAM maturity assessments.
- Conducts internal audits of IAM controls.
- Tracks remediation activities.
- Maintains an IAM strategic roadmap.
- Implements automation initiatives.
- Reviews lessons learned.
- Reports IAM performance to executive leadership.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to continuously improve IAM may result in:

- Stagnant security posture.
- Repeated audit findings.
- Ineffective remediation.
- Increased cyber risk.
- Regulatory noncompliance.
- Slow response to emerging threats.
- Reduced operational efficiency.
- Weak executive visibility.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Continuous improvement embedded in governance.
- KPIs and KRIs actively managed.
- Audits performed regularly.
- Strategic roadmap maintained.
- Executive oversight strong.

### Partially Effective

- Improvement program established.
- Metrics available.
- Some remediation delays.
- Automation opportunities remain.

### Ineffective

- No formal improvement program.
- KPIs not tracked.
- Audits infrequent.
- Remediation unmanaged.
- Executive reporting absent.
