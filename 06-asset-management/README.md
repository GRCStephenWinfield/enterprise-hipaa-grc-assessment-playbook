# 06 – Asset Management

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The Asset Management domain evaluates the organization's ability to identify, inventory, classify, manage, monitor, protect, and retire enterprise technology assets throughout their lifecycle.

This assessment examines governance, ownership, inventory accuracy, asset classification, lifecycle management, software licensing, cloud assets, medical devices, shadow IT, and continuous improvement to determine the maturity of the organization's Asset Management program.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to determine whether enterprise assets are properly governed, inventoried, classified, protected, monitored, and managed throughout their lifecycle.

The assessment evaluates:

- Enterprise Hardware Inventory
- Software Asset Management
- Cloud Asset Inventory
- Medical Device Asset Management
- Asset Ownership
- Asset Classification
- Configuration Management
- Asset Lifecycle Management
- Asset Discovery
- Shadow IT Identification
- Secure Asset Disposal
- Asset Monitoring
- Software License Management
- Asset Risk Integration
- Continuous Improvement
