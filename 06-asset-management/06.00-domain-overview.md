# Purpose

The Asset Management assessment evaluates Magnolia Regional Medical Center's ability to identify, inventory, classify, assign ownership, protect, monitor, and manage information assets throughout their lifecycle.

This assessment includes physical assets, virtual assets, cloud resources, medical devices, applications, data repositories, software licenses, and supporting infrastructure.

Effective asset management provides the foundation for cybersecurity governance, risk management, vulnerability management, incident response, disaster recovery, and regulatory compliance.

---

# Assessment Objectives

The objectives of this assessment are to determine whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center:

- Maintains a complete enterprise asset inventory.
- Assigns ownership to critical assets.
- Classifies information assets appropriately.
- Maintains accurate software inventories.
- Identifies cloud-hosted assets.
- Identifies Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) devices.
- Maintains configuration management records.
- Tracks asset lifecycle activities.
- Removes obsolete assets securely.
- Continuously updates asset inventories.
