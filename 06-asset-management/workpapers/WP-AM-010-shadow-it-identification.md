# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center identifies, investigates, and governs unauthorized technology deployed outside approved IT governance processes.

The assessment determines whether unauthorized hardware, software, SaaS applications, cloud resources, AI services, mobile applications, browser extensions, and other technology are detected and managed to reduce cybersecurity, operational, and regulatory risk.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Detects Shadow IT.
- Investigates unauthorized technology.
- Maintains governance over cloud applications.
- Identifies unauthorized AI services.
- Reviews unauthorized SaaS usage.
- Documents exceptions.
- Reduces organizational exposure created by unmanaged technology.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to govern Shadow IT may result in:

- Unauthorized SaaS applications.
- Unapproved AI services processing sensitive data.
- Unauthorized cloud storage.
- Data leakage.
- ePHI exposure.
- Licensing violations.
- Incomplete asset inventories.
- Increased attack surface.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Continuous Shadow IT detection.
- AI usage governed.
- Unauthorized technology investigated.
- Exceptions documented.
- Governance integrated into security operations.

### Partially Effective

- Shadow IT identified.
- Limited AI governance.
- Some investigations delayed.
- Inconsistent reporting.

### Ineffective

- No Shadow IT monitoring.
- Unauthorized AI widespread.
- SaaS usage unmanaged.
- No governance process.
- High organizational exposure.
