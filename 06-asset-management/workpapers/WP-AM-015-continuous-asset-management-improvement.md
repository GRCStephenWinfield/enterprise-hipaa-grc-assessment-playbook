# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center continuously measures, reviews, and improves the effectiveness of its Asset Management program.

The assessment determines whether management uses performance metrics, risk indicators, internal audits, lessons learned, corrective actions, and maturity assessments to drive continual improvement.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Measures Asset Management performance.
- Maintains Asset Management KPIs and KRIs.
- Performs periodic management reviews.
- Conducts internal audits.
- Tracks corrective actions.
- Performs maturity assessments.
- Continuously improves the Asset Management program.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to continuously improve Asset Management may result in:

- Repeated control deficiencies.
- Asset inventory degradation.
- Unknown security gaps.
- Poor executive visibility.
- Ineffective governance.
- Regulatory findings.
- Increased operational risk.
- Stagnant cybersecurity maturity.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- KPIs and KRIs actively monitored.
- Internal audits performed.
- Management reviews occur regularly.
- Improvement initiatives implemented.
- Maturity assessments completed annually.

### Partially Effective

- Performance metrics maintained.
- Improvement activities inconsistent.
- Limited executive review.
- Corrective actions delayed.

### Ineffective

- No improvement program.
- KPIs unavailable.
- Internal audits absent.
- No management reviews.
- Maturity not measured.
