# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center securely sanitizes, destroys, and disposes of enterprise assets and storage media in accordance with organizational policy, regulatory requirements, and industry best practices.

The assessment determines whether sensitive information is rendered unrecoverable before assets are transferred, reused, recycled, or destroyed.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Maintains secure asset disposal procedures.
- Sanitizes storage media prior to disposal or reuse.
- Documents disposal activities.
- Maintains chain-of-custody records.
- Oversees third-party disposal vendors.
- Retains certificates of destruction.
- Verifies disposal effectiveness.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to securely dispose of assets may result in:

- Recovery of sensitive information.
- Exposure of ePHI.
- Data breaches.
- Regulatory violations.
- Unauthorized asset reuse.
- Loss of chain of custody.
- Reputational damage.
- Increased legal liability.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Disposal procedures documented.
- NIST SP 800-88 followed.
- Complete documentation retained.
- Vendor oversight effective.
- Chain of custody maintained.

### Partially Effective

- Disposal process exists.
- Minor documentation gaps.
- Vendor oversight inconsistent.
- Some sanitization records incomplete.

### Ineffective

- Disposal undocumented.
- Media not sanitized.
- Certificates missing.
- Vendor oversight absent.
- Chain of custody incomplete.
