# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a complete, accurate, and continuously updated inventory of enterprise hardware assets.

The assessment determines whether all physical and virtual hardware assets are identified, assigned ownership, classified appropriately, monitored throughout their lifecycle, and reconciled with authoritative inventory sources.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Maintains a complete enterprise hardware inventory.
- Assigns ownership to hardware assets.
- Maintains accurate asset attributes.
- Reconciles inventory with authoritative sources.
- Identifies unmanaged devices.
- Monitors inventory changes.
- Supports cybersecurity operations through accurate asset records.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to maintain an accurate hardware inventory may result in:

- Unknown assets connected to the network.
- Missed security patches.
- Incomplete vulnerability scanning.
- Unauthorized devices.
- Incident response delays.
- Regulatory noncompliance.
- Increased attack surface.
- Asset accountability gaps.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Complete hardware inventory.
- Accurate asset attributes.
- Regular reconciliation.
- Assigned ownership.
- Continuous updates.
- Low inventory discrepancies.

### Partially Effective

- Inventory exists.
- Minor inaccuracies.
- Reconciliation inconsistent.
- Some ownership gaps.

### Ineffective

- Incomplete inventory.
- Significant inaccuracies.
- No reconciliation process.
- Unassigned assets.
- Unknown hardware present.
