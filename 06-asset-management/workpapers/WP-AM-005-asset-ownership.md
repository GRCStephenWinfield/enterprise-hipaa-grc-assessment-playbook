# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center assigns appropriate ownership for enterprise assets throughout their lifecycle.

The assessment determines whether business owners, technical owners, and custodians have been assigned for enterprise hardware, software, cloud resources, medical devices, applications, databases, and information assets.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Assigns business ownership.
- Assigns technical ownership.
- Assigns custodians where appropriate.
- Documents ownership responsibilities.
- Reviews ownership periodically.
- Updates ownership following personnel or organizational changes.
- Integrates ownership into governance processes.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to assign ownership may result in:

- Assets without accountability.
- Delayed vulnerability remediation.
- Inconsistent patch management.
- Unclear incident response responsibilities.
- Weak lifecycle governance.
- Regulatory noncompliance.
- Increased operational risk.
- Poor executive accountability.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Ownership assigned for all critical assets.
- Responsibilities documented.
- Ownership regularly reviewed.
- Governance integration established.
- Personnel changes reflected promptly.

### Partially Effective

- Ownership assigned for most assets.
- Minor documentation gaps.
- Reviews performed inconsistently.
- Some ownership records outdated.

### Ineffective

- Numerous assets lack ownership.
- Responsibilities undefined.
- No review process.
- Ownership records inaccurate or outdated.
