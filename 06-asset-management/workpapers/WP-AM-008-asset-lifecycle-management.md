# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center effectively manages enterprise assets throughout their entire lifecycle, from acquisition through deployment, maintenance, transfer, reuse, and secure retirement.

The assessment determines whether lifecycle activities are documented, governed, monitored, and integrated into enterprise cybersecurity and operational processes.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Governs asset acquisition.
- Controls deployment processes.
- Maintains lifecycle records.
- Reviews assets throughout operational use.
- Manages transfers and reassignment.
- Securely retires assets.
- Maintains complete lifecycle documentation.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to manage asset lifecycles may result in:

- Lost assets.
- Unauthorized reuse.
- Data exposure.
- Unsupported systems.
- Missing inventory records.
- Weak accountability.
- Regulatory noncompliance.
- Increased cybersecurity risk.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Lifecycle fully documented.
- Governance enforced.
- Inventory updated throughout lifecycle.
- Secure retirement documented.
- Accountability maintained.

### Partially Effective

- Lifecycle documented.
- Minor record gaps.
- Some inventory delays.
- Inconsistent transfer documentation.

### Ineffective

- Lifecycle undocumented.
- Inventory inaccurate.
- Transfers unmanaged.
- Retirement undocumented.
- Disposal evidence missing.
