# Purpose

This Risk Register documents the enterprise cybersecurity risks identified during the Asset Management assessment.

Each risk has been evaluated based on likelihood, business impact, inherent risk, residual risk, ownership, and recommended treatment strategy.

This register supports executive decision-making, enterprise risk governance, and prioritization of remediation efforts.

# Highest Priority Risks

The following enterprise risks require immediate executive attention:

### RSK-AM-004 — Unsupported Medical Device Firmware

**Business Impact**

Unsupported medical device firmware increases the likelihood of cybersecurity compromise, operational disruption, patient safety events, and regulatory noncompliance.

---

### RSK-AM-002 — Unsupported Software

**Business Impact**

Unsupported software may expose enterprise systems to unpatched vulnerabilities, ransomware, and operational instability.

---

### RSK-AM-010 — Shadow IT

**Business Impact**

Unauthorized SaaS applications and AI services increase the risk of unauthorized data disclosure, ePHI exposure, and compliance violations.

---

### RSK-AM-009 — Delayed Asset Reconciliation

**Business Impact**

Delayed synchronization between discovered assets and the CMDB reduces visibility and weakens vulnerability management and incident response.

---

### RSK-AM-012 — Incomplete Asset Monitoring

**Business Impact**

Incomplete monitoring coverage may delay detection of unauthorized changes affecting critical enterprise and medical assets.
