# 12 – Physical & Environmental Security

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The Physical & Environmental Security (PES) domain evaluates the organization's ability to protect personnel, facilities, critical infrastructure, clinical environments, and technology assets from physical threats, environmental hazards, and operational disruptions.

This assessment examines physical security governance, facility access controls, surveillance, environmental monitoring, power resilience, fire protection, equipment security, medical device protection, emergency response, and continuous improvement.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to determine whether physical and environmental security controls effectively protect organizational assets, ensure personnel safety, maintain regulatory compliance, and support operational resilience.

The assessment evaluates:

- Physical Security Governance
- Facility Perimeter Security
- Restricted Areas
- Badge & Key Management
- Visitor Management
- CCTV & Surveillance
- Data Center Security
- Server Room Security
- Environmental Monitoring
- Power & UPS Resilience
- Generator & Fuel Management
- HVAC & Cooling
- Fire Detection & Suppression
- Medical Device Physical Security
- Loading Dock & Mailroom Security
- Physical Incident Response
- Continuous Improvement
