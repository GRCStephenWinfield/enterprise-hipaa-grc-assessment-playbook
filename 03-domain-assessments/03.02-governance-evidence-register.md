# Purpose

The Governance Evidence Register serves as the authoritative inventory of all evidence requested, received, reviewed, and evaluated during the Governance Domain Assessment.

The register establishes complete traceability between evidence, control objectives, framework requirements, workpapers, findings, risks, and recommendations.

Every piece of evidence received from Magnolia Regional Medical Center shall be assigned a unique Evidence ID and tracked through the completion of the engagement.

---

# Objectives

This register is designed to:

- Track all requested governance evidence.
- Monitor evidence collection status.
- Ensure evidence completeness.
- Maintain chain of custody.
- Support workpaper development.
- Enable quality assurance reviews.
- Provide full assessment traceability.

---

# Evidence Identification Standard

Each evidence item shall receive a permanent identifier.

Format:

```text
EVD-GOV-###
```

Examples:

```text
EVD-GOV-001
EVD-GOV-002
EVD-GOV-003
```

Evidence IDs remain permanent throughout the assessment.

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Assign Evidence IDs before receiving documentation.
- Maintain one centralized evidence inventory.
- Review evidence immediately upon receipt.
- Request missing information early.
- Link every evidence item to its corresponding workpaper.

## Common Pitfalls

- Receiving evidence without assigning an ID.
- Storing duplicate copies.
- Reviewing evidence without recording metadata.
- Delaying follow-up requests.
- Losing traceability between evidence and findings.

## Red Flags

- Evidence submitted after fieldwork closes.
- Documents without approval signatures.
- Outdated governance policies.
- Missing board reporting artifacts.
- Risk registers not updated within the last year.
- Governance committee minutes showing poor attendance or lack of cybersecurity discussion.
