# Purpose

This workpaper documents testing performed to determine whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center has established, approved, communicated, maintained, and periodically reviewed enterprise information security policies that support the protection of electronic protected health information (ePHI) and comply with applicable regulatory and industry requirements.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization maintains an effective Information Security Policy Management Program that establishes governance expectations, assigns ownership, supports regulatory compliance, and guides operational security activities.

# Risk Statement

Without formally approved, regularly reviewed, and effectively communicated security policies, personnel may perform security-related activities inconsistently, increasing the likelihood of unauthorized disclosure, modification, or loss of ePHI.

---

# Control Description

The organization should maintain an enterprise policy management program that includes:

- Executive approval
- Defined ownership
- Scheduled reviews
- Version control
- Employee accessibility
- Regulatory alignment
- Document retention
- Change history

---

# Testing Procedures

The following procedures were performed:

1. Obtained the enterprise Information Security Policy.
2. Verified executive approval.
3. Confirmed policy ownership.
4. Reviewed version history.
5. Confirmed annual review schedule.
6. Verified policy distribution process.
7. Reviewed policy exception process.
8. Compared policy requirements with HIPAA Security Rule requirements.
9. Validated interview responses.
10. Evaluated policy management maturity.

# Results

The organization maintains a comprehensive Information Security Policy approved by executive leadership.

Testing confirmed:

- Executive approval documented.
- Policy owner assigned.
- Annual review schedule established.
- Version history maintained.
- Regulatory references included.
- Policy exceptions documented.
- Supporting standards exist.
- Procedures align with policy requirements.

Review of policy artifacts demonstrated that governance requirements are communicated consistently throughout the organization.

## Improvement Opportunities

While policies are reviewed annually, performance metrics related to policy effectiveness are limited.

Examples of additional governance metrics include:

- Policy exception trends.
- Policy acknowledgement completion rates.
- Overdue policy reviews.
- Policy compliance metrics.
- Business-unit compliance reporting.
