# Purpose

This workpaper documents testing performed to determine whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains an effective governance process for coordinating cybersecurity activities with the Internal Audit function.

The assessment evaluates audit independence, planning, reporting, follow-up procedures, remediation governance, and executive oversight of cybersecurity audit findings.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether Internal Audit provides independent assurance over the cybersecurity program and whether audit findings are effectively governed through remediation, validation, escalation, and executive reporting.

# Risk Statement

Without effective coordination between Internal Audit and the cybersecurity program, governance deficiencies may remain unidentified, corrective actions may not be validated, and executive leadership may not receive independent assurance regarding cybersecurity risk management.

---

# Control Description

The organization should maintain a governance process that:

- Preserves Internal Audit independence.
- Conducts annual cybersecurity audits.
- Tracks remediation activities.
- Validates corrective actions.
- Escalates overdue findings.
- Reports audit status to executive leadership.
- Coordinates audit planning with enterprise risks.

---

# Testing Procedures

The following procedures were performed:

1. Reviewed the Internal Audit Charter.
2. Examined the annual audit plan.
3. Reviewed cybersecurity audit reports.
4. Evaluated audit independence.
5. Reviewed remediation tracking.
6. Examined validation procedures.
7. Reviewed executive reporting.
8. Compared audit findings with enterprise risks.
9. Interviewed Internal Audit leadership.
10. Assessed overall audit governance maturity.

# Results

Testing confirmed that Internal Audit operates independently and performs scheduled cybersecurity audits based upon enterprise risk.

Evidence demonstrated:

- Annual audit plans approved.
- Cybersecurity included within audit scope.
- Findings communicated to executive leadership.
- Corrective actions assigned.
- Audit status reported quarterly.
- Executive oversight documented.

Testing identified one improvement opportunity involving independent validation of corrective actions before closure.

---

# Assessor Observations

## Positive Observations

✓ Internal Audit independence maintained.

✓ Risk-based audit planning implemented.

✓ Executive reporting established.

✓ Strong collaboration between Internal Audit and Information Security.

✓ Audit findings centrally tracked.

✓ Audit plans reviewed annually.

---

## Improvement Opportunities

Strengthen audit follow-up governance by:

- Performing documented validation testing before closing findings.
- Standardizing validation procedures.
- Recording validation evidence.
- Including validation status within executive dashboards.

---

# Exceptions Identified

No material control deficiencies identified.

One governance process improvement documented.

---

# Root Cause Analysis

Corrective actions are reviewed by management before closure; however, independent validation procedures are not consistently documented.

---

# Business Impact

Independent validation strengthens confidence that corrective actions effectively address the original finding and reduces the likelihood of recurring audit observations.

No evidence suggested ineffective remediation.
