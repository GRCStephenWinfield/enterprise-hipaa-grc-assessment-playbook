# Purpose

This workpaper documents testing performed to determine whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center has established an effective governance process for identifying, evaluating, implementing, monitoring, and reporting cybersecurity regulatory requirements applicable to the organization.

The assessment focuses on governance over HIPAA Security Rule compliance while also evaluating oversight of other applicable federal and state cybersecurity obligations.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether executive leadership provides effective oversight of regulatory compliance activities through documented governance processes, accountability, monitoring, reporting, and continuous improvement.

# Risk Statement

Without effective regulatory governance, the organization may fail to identify or implement new compliance requirements, increasing the likelihood of regulatory findings, enforcement actions, financial penalties, and unauthorized disclosure of ePHI.

---

# Control Description

The organization should maintain a regulatory governance process that includes:

- Regulatory monitoring
- Compliance ownership
- Regulatory change assessments
- Policy updates
- Compliance reporting
- Internal compliance reviews
- Executive oversight
- Corrective action monitoring
- Audit readiness

---

# Testing Procedures

The following procedures were performed:

1. Reviewed the Regulatory Compliance Program.
2. Examined HIPAA compliance dashboards.
3. Reviewed regulatory change procedures.
4. Verified compliance ownership assignments.
5. Examined recent compliance assessments.
6. Reviewed executive compliance reports.
7. Evaluated corrective-action tracking.
8. Compared compliance activities with interview responses.
9. Reviewed regulatory monitoring activities.
10. Assessed overall compliance governance maturity.

# Results

Testing confirmed that Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a mature compliance governance program.

Evidence demonstrated:

- HIPAA compliance ownership assigned.
- Monthly compliance reporting performed.
- Executive oversight established.
- Internal assessments completed.
- Corrective actions tracked.
- Compliance metrics reported.

Testing identified one opportunity regarding documentation of regulatory change analysis.

While regulatory updates were monitored, documentation explaining how individual regulatory changes were analyzed and incorporated into governance processes was inconsistent.

---

# Assessor Observations

## Positive Observations

✓ Dedicated Compliance Office.

✓ Strong Privacy and Security coordination.

✓ Executive compliance reporting.

✓ HIPAA governance integrated into enterprise governance.

✓ Internal compliance reviews completed regularly.

✓ Corrective-action process established.

---

## Improvement Opportunities

Strengthen regulatory change management by:

- Standardizing regulatory impact assessments.
- Recording implementation decisions.
- Maintaining regulatory decision logs.
- Tracking implementation status.
- Documenting executive approval of significant regulatory changes.

---

# Exceptions Identified

## Exception GOV-011-01

Documentation supporting formal analysis of regulatory changes was inconsistent across sampled updates.

---

# Root Cause Analysis

The organization effectively monitors regulatory developments but has not standardized documentation describing the impact assessment performed before implementing governance changes.

---

# Business Impact

Insufficient documentation of regulatory analysis may:

- Reduce audit defensibility.
- Delay implementation of regulatory changes.
- Create inconsistent interpretation.
- Increase regulatory examination findings.

No evidence indicated that required regulatory changes were missed.

# Finding Reference

**Title**

Regulatory Change Impact Assessments Are Not Consistently Documented

**Severity**

**Low**

---

# Risk Reference

## RSK-GOV-006

Incomplete documentation of regulatory impact assessments may reduce the organization's ability to demonstrate due diligence during regulatory examinations.

---

# Recommendation Reference

## REC-GOV-006

Implement a standardized Regulatory Change Assessment template that documents:

- Regulatory source
- Business impact
- Technical impact
- Required policy changes
- Responsible owner
- Executive approval
- Implementation timeline
- Validation date

---

# Proposed Management Response

## MR-GOV-006

Management agrees with the recommendation and will implement a standardized Regulatory Change Assessment process integrated with the existing compliance governance workflow.

Target Completion:

**Q3 FY2027**

Responsible Owner:

**Chief Compliance Officer**

---

# Workpaper Conclusion

Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains an effective regulatory compliance governance program with strong executive oversight and mature HIPAA compliance processes.

The identified observation relates to documentation consistency rather than regulatory compliance effectiveness.

Implementation of standardized regulatory impact assessments will improve governance maturity and audit readiness.
