# Purpose

This workpaper documents testing performed to determine whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center has established and maintains a formally approved Cybersecurity Governance Charter that clearly defines governance objectives, authority, accountability, organizational responsibilities, and executive oversight.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether executive leadership has formally established cybersecurity governance and assigned accountability for the cybersecurity program.

# Risk Statement

Without an approved governance charter, cybersecurity responsibilities may be unclear, executive oversight may be inconsistent, and organizational accountability may not support effective management of risks affecting ePHI.

---

# Control Description

The organization should maintain a formally approved Governance Charter that defines:

- governance structure
- executive authority
- reporting relationships
- committee responsibilities
- program objectives
- decision authority
- policy ownership

---

# Testing Procedures

The following procedures were performed.

1. Obtained the Information Security Program Charter.

2. Verified executive approval.

3. Confirmed document version.

4. Reviewed governance responsibilities.

5. Reviewed reporting structure.

6. Verified governance committee references.

7. Confirmed annual review requirement.

8. Compared charter against interview responses.

9. Compared charter against organizational chart.

10. Evaluated alignment with NIST CSF Govern Function.

# Results

The Governance Charter was reviewed and determined to be formally approved by executive leadership.

The charter establishes:

- executive sponsorship
- governance committee authority
- cybersecurity objectives
- reporting responsibilities
- policy ownership
- accountability structure

Minor improvement opportunities were identified regarding periodic charter performance metrics.

---

# Assessor Observations

Positive

✓ Executive approval documented

✓ Governance ownership defined

✓ Annual review established

✓ Clear reporting structure

✓ Charter aligns with enterprise strategy

Improvement Opportunity

The charter references governance reporting but does not define measurable governance performance indicators.
