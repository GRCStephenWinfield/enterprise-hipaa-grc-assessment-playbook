# Governance Workpaper Index

> **Phase 03 – Governance Domain Assessment**

This folder contains the detailed control-testing workpapers for the Governance assessment.

Each workpaper documents the control objective, framework alignment, evidence requirements, testing procedures, observations, risk assessment, maturity evaluation, findings, and conclusion for a specific Governance capability.

---

## Governance Workpapers

| Workpaper | Assessment Area | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [WP-GOV-001](WP-GOV-001-governance-charter.md) | Governance Charter | Evaluates the formal governance charter, authority, scope, approval, and accountability structure. |
| [WP-GOV-002](WP-GOV-002-executive-oversight.md) | Executive Oversight | Evaluates executive sponsorship, leadership involvement, escalation, and management oversight. |
| [WP-GOV-003](WP-GOV-003-policy-management.md) | Policy Management | Evaluates policy ownership, approval, communication, review, versioning, and enforcement. |
| [WP-GOV-004](WP-GOV-004-roles-responsibilities.md) | Roles and Responsibilities | Evaluates role clarity, accountability, segregation of duties, and delegated authority. |
| [WP-GOV-005](WP-GOV-005-security-strategy.md) | Security Strategy | Evaluates strategic alignment, objectives, roadmap, priorities, and progress monitoring. |
| [WP-GOV-006](WP-GOV-006-security-metrics.md) | Security Metrics | Evaluates KPI, KRI, KCI, target, threshold, data-quality, and reporting practices. |
| [WP-GOV-007](WP-GOV-007-enterprise-risk-governance.md) | Enterprise Risk Governance | Evaluates integration between cybersecurity governance and enterprise risk management. |
| [WP-GOV-008](WP-GOV-008-security-budget-governance.md) | Security Budget Governance | Evaluates cybersecurity budgeting, investment prioritization, business cases, and oversight. |
| [WP-GOV-009](WP-GOV-009-governance-committee-operations.md) | Governance Committee Operations | Evaluates committee charters, membership, cadence, agendas, decisions, actions, and escalation. |
| [WP-GOV-010](WP-GOV-010-exception-management.md) | Exception Management | Evaluates policy exceptions, waivers, compensating controls, approvals, expiration, and review. |
| [WP-GOV-011](WP-GOV-011-regulatory-compliance.md) | Regulatory Compliance | Evaluates oversight of legal, regulatory, contractual, and industry requirements. |
| [WP-GOV-012](WP-GOV-012-policy-review-process.md) | Policy Review Process | Evaluates scheduled reviews, stakeholder participation, approvals, and policy maintenance. |
| [WP-GOV-013](WP-GOV-013-internal-audit-coordination.md) | Internal Audit Coordination | Evaluates coordination with Internal Audit, issue tracking, assurance, and corrective-action oversight. |
| [WP-GOV-014](WP-GOV-014-board-cybersecurity-reporting.md) | Board Cybersecurity Reporting | Evaluates Board reporting, risk communication, trends, decisions, and governance oversight. |
| [WP-GOV-015](WP-GOV-015-continuous-governance-improvement.md) | Continuous Governance Improvement | Evaluates maturity assessment, lessons learned, benchmarking, roadmap management, and sustained improvement. |

## Standard Workpaper Components

Each workpaper includes:

- Executive Summary
- Business Objective
- Control Objective
- Framework Mapping
- Inherent Risk
- Expected Evidence
- Testing Procedures
- Sample Validation
- Control Effectiveness Rating
- Risk Assessment
- Interview Notes
- Auditor Observations
- Strengths and Weaknesses
- Potential Findings
- Maturity Assessment
- Traceability
- Conclusion
- Quality Assurance Checklist
