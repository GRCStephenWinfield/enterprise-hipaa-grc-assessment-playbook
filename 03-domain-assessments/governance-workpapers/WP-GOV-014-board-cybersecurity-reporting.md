# Purpose

This workpaper documents testing performed to determine whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center provides the Board of Directors with sufficient cybersecurity reporting to enable informed governance, strategic oversight, enterprise risk management, and fulfillment of fiduciary responsibilities.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether cybersecurity information presented to the Board enables effective oversight of enterprise cyber risk, strategic initiatives, regulatory obligations, organizational resilience, and cybersecurity program maturity.

# Risk Statement

Without effective Board cybersecurity reporting, directors may lack sufficient information to oversee enterprise cyber risk, allocate resources appropriately, evaluate organizational resilience, and fulfill governance responsibilities related to the protection of electronic protected health information (ePHI).

---

# Control Description

Board cybersecurity reporting should include:

- Enterprise cyber risk posture
- Significant emerging threats
- Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Risk appetite status
- Security maturity
- Regulatory compliance
- Incident trends
- Third-party cyber risk
- Major remediation initiatives
- Budget performance
- Strategic roadmap progress

---

# Testing Procedures

The following procedures were performed:

1. Reviewed Board cybersecurity presentation materials.
2. Examined quarterly Board meeting minutes.
3. Compared Board reports with executive dashboards.
4. Evaluated strategic versus operational reporting.
5. Verified Board reporting frequency.
6. Reviewed Board action items.
7. Compared reporting with enterprise risks.
8. Interviewed executive leadership.
9. Evaluated governance effectiveness.
10. Assessed Board reporting maturity.

# Results

Testing confirmed that cybersecurity is presented to the Board on a quarterly basis.

Evidence demonstrated:

- Quarterly Board reporting established.
- Executive leadership participates in presentations.
- Significant cybersecurity initiatives are discussed.
- Major incidents are reported.
- HIPAA compliance updates are included.
- Budget requests are presented.

However, Board reporting focuses primarily on operational activity rather than strategic enterprise risk.

Several reports lacked:

- Enterprise Key Risk Indicators (KRIs)
- Risk appetite reporting
- Residual risk trends
- Cybersecurity maturity trend analysis
- Third-party cyber risk summaries
- Business impact analysis

---

# Assessor Observations

## Positive Observations

✓ Cybersecurity receives regular Board attention.

✓ Executive participation is strong.

✓ Significant incidents are communicated.

✓ Budget discussions occur regularly.

✓ Strategic initiatives are presented.

---

## Improvement Opportunities

Board reporting should evolve toward enterprise governance by incorporating:

- Risk appetite dashboards
- Enterprise KRIs
- Residual risk trend analysis
- Third-party cyber risk exposure
- Cybersecurity maturity progression
- Regulatory risk indicators
- Financial impact of cyber risk
- Industry benchmarking

---

# Exceptions Identified

## Exception GOV-014-01

Board cybersecurity reporting emphasizes operational security activities but provides limited information regarding enterprise cyber risk, strategic risk exposure, and long-term program maturity.

---

# Root Cause Analysis

Board reporting evolved from executive operational dashboards.

Reporting has not been redesigned specifically for Board governance responsibilities and strategic decision-making.

---

# Business Impact

Without strategic cybersecurity reporting:

- Directors may have limited visibility into enterprise cyber risk.
- Resource allocation decisions may rely upon incomplete information.
- Risk appetite oversight becomes more difficult.
- Long-term governance effectiveness may be reduced.

No evidence indicated inadequate Board engagement.

# Recommendation Reference

## REC-GOV-009

Redesign Board cybersecurity reporting to include:

- Enterprise Key Risk Indicators
- Risk appetite status
- Residual risk trends
- Cybersecurity maturity scorecards
- Third-party cyber risk
- Financial exposure estimates
- Strategic initiative performance
- Industry benchmarking

---

# Proposed Management Response

## MR-GOV-009

Management agrees with the recommendation.

A redesigned Board Cybersecurity Dashboard will be implemented using strategic governance metrics beginning with the next fiscal reporting cycle.

Target Completion:

Responsible Owner:

**Chief Information Security Officer**

# Workpaper Conclusion

Magnolia Regional Medical Center demonstrates strong executive engagement and regular Board communication regarding cybersecurity.

The assessment identified a governance maturity opportunity to improve the strategic value of Board reporting.

Expanding Board reporting to include enterprise cyber risk analytics, strategic performance measures, and governance-focused metrics will significantly improve Board oversight and organizational resilience.
