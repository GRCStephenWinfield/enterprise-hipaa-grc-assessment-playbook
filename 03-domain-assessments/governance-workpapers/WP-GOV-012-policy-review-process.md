# Purpose

This workpaper documents testing performed to determine whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains an effective governance process for reviewing, approving, updating, communicating, and retiring enterprise cybersecurity policies.

The assessment evaluates the lifecycle management of security policies to ensure they remain current, aligned with regulatory requirements, and reflective of organizational risk.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether cybersecurity policies are reviewed according to a documented schedule, approved by appropriate leadership, updated when necessary, and communicated throughout the organization.

# Risk Statement

Without an effective policy review process, cybersecurity policies may become outdated, inconsistent with regulatory requirements, or misaligned with current business operations and emerging threats.

---

# Control Description

The organization should maintain a formal policy lifecycle that includes:

- Assigned policy owner
- Review frequency
- Executive approval
- Version control
- Regulatory review
- Stakeholder review
- Communication
- Retirement process
- Document retention

---

# Testing Procedures

The following procedures were performed:

1. Reviewed the enterprise policy review schedule.
2. Verified policy ownership assignments.
3. Examined version history.
4. Reviewed policy approval records.
5. Evaluated overdue policies.
6. Compared policy updates with regulatory changes.
7. Reviewed communication records.
8. Interviewed policy owners.
9. Reviewed retired policy archive.
10. Assessed policy lifecycle maturity.

# Results

Testing confirmed that Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a documented policy review program.

Evidence demonstrated:

- Every policy has an assigned owner.
- Annual review requirements are established.
- Executive approvals are documented.
- Version history is maintained.
- Previous versions are archived.
- Policy changes are communicated to affected stakeholders.

One policy exceeded its annual review date by eighteen days before being approved.

The delay did not result in noncompliance, and no material regulatory changes occurred during the review period.

---

# Assessor Observations

## Positive Observations

✓ Mature policy governance process.

✓ Executive approvals consistently documented.

✓ Excellent document version control.

✓ Policies archived appropriately.

✓ Strong ownership model.

✓ Annual review schedule maintained.

---

## Improvement Opportunities

Implement automated workflow notifications to:

- Alert policy owners before review deadlines.
- Escalate overdue reviews.
- Track review completion metrics.
- Measure average policy approval time.

# Exceptions Identified

No material control deficiencies identified.

# Root Cause Analysis

# Business Impact
