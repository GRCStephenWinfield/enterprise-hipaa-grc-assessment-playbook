# Purpose

This workpaper documents testing performed to determine whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center has established, approved, communicated, and periodically updated an Enterprise Cybersecurity Strategy that aligns cybersecurity initiatives with organizational objectives, patient safety, enterprise risk management, and applicable regulatory requirements.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether executive leadership maintains a documented cybersecurity strategy that defines long-term security objectives, aligns investments with organizational priorities, and supports continuous improvement of the cybersecurity program.

# Risk Statement

Without a documented cybersecurity strategy aligned to business objectives, the organization may prioritize security investments inconsistently, fail to address emerging threats, and experience gaps in regulatory compliance, operational resilience, and protection of ePHI.

---

# Control Description

The organization should maintain a formal cybersecurity strategy that:

- Aligns with enterprise objectives.
- Supports patient safety.
- Defines strategic initiatives.
- Establishes measurable goals.
- Aligns with enterprise risk management.
- Supports regulatory compliance.
- Defines investment priorities.
- Is reviewed annually by executive leadership.

---

# Testing Procedures

The following procedures were performed:

1. Obtained the Enterprise Cybersecurity Strategy.
2. Verified executive approval.
3. Reviewed strategic planning horizon.
4. Evaluated alignment with organizational goals.
5. Verified linkage to enterprise risk management.
6. Reviewed strategic initiative tracking.
7. Compared strategic objectives with annual security budget.
8. Validated strategy through executive interviews.
9. Evaluated governance oversight of strategic initiatives.
10. Assessed overall strategy maturity.

# Results

Testing confirmed that Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a formally documented Enterprise Cybersecurity Strategy approved by executive leadership.

The strategy includes:

- Vision and mission
- Strategic cybersecurity objectives
- Multi-year roadmap
- Enterprise risk alignment
- HIPAA compliance objectives
- Security modernization initiatives
- Budget priorities
- Executive governance oversight

Testing also confirmed that strategic initiatives are reviewed quarterly by executive leadership.

---

# Assessor Observations

## Positive Observations

✓ Executive-approved cybersecurity strategy.

✓ Clear linkage between cybersecurity and patient safety.

✓ Multi-year strategic roadmap established.

✓ Strategic initiatives prioritized by business risk.

✓ Executive oversight documented.

✓ Funding priorities support strategic objectives.

---

## Improvement Opportunities

The strategy identifies measurable objectives; however, it does not consistently define quantitative success measures for every strategic initiative.

Examples include:

- Target cybersecurity maturity levels.
- Risk reduction targets.
- Security control coverage goals.
- Third-party risk reduction objectives.
- Security awareness effectiveness targets.

---

# Exceptions Identified

No material deficiencies identified.

One strategic planning enhancement opportunity documented.

---

# Root Cause Analysis

The current strategy emphasizes initiative completion rather than measurable business outcomes.

As the cybersecurity program matures, additional performance metrics should be incorporated into future strategy updates.

---

# Business Impact

The current strategy effectively guides cybersecurity investments and governance activities.

Expanding measurable performance objectives would improve executive decision-making, accountability, and long-term program evaluation.

# Recommendations

To further mature the cybersecurity strategy:

- Define measurable success criteria for each strategic initiative.
- Establish annual cybersecurity maturity targets.
- Incorporate enterprise Key Risk Indicators (KRIs).
- Develop executive scorecards tied to strategic objectives.
- Benchmark cybersecurity performance against peer healthcare organizations.

---

# Workpaper Conclusion

Magnolia Regional Medical Center has established a well-defined Enterprise Cybersecurity Strategy that aligns cybersecurity initiatives with organizational priorities, patient safety, and regulatory obligations.

The strategy demonstrates executive commitment and effective governance. Incorporating additional measurable performance objectives will strengthen strategic oversight and support continued program maturity.
