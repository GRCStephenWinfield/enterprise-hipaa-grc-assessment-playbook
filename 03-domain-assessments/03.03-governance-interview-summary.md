# Purpose

This document summarizes interviews conducted with executive leadership and governance stakeholders during the Governance Domain Assessment.

The purpose of these interviews is to validate governance practices, confirm operational implementation, corroborate documentary evidence, identify inconsistencies, and gather additional context regarding the organization's cybersecurity governance program.

Interview information is one component of the assessment and is corroborated through documentary evidence, control testing, and technical validation before conclusions are reached.

---

# Interview Objectives

The Governance interviews are intended to determine whether:

- Executive leadership actively governs cybersecurity.
- Governance responsibilities are clearly assigned.
- Policies are implemented consistently.
- Enterprise cyber risks are discussed at the executive level.
- Governance committees operate effectively.
- Budget decisions align with cybersecurity strategy.
- Regulatory obligations are actively managed.
- Security metrics support executive decision-making.
- Continuous improvement processes exist.

# Executive Interview Summary

Overall observations indicate that Magnolia Regional Medical Center has established executive governance structures and demonstrates leadership commitment to cybersecurity. Executive stakeholders consistently described cybersecurity as an enterprise risk rather than solely an IT responsibility.

Interview responses generally aligned with the Information Security Program Charter (EVD-GOV-001), Governance Committee Charter (EVD-GOV-002), and Enterprise Cybersecurity Strategy (EVD-GOV-005).

Several opportunities for improvement were identified regarding governance metrics, formal risk acceptance documentation, and board reporting consistency.

## Interview Summary

The CISO stated that cybersecurity governance is coordinated through a quarterly Cybersecurity Governance Committee chaired by executive leadership.

The committee reviews:

- Enterprise cyber risks
- HIPAA compliance
- Major initiatives
- Security metrics
- Budget requests
- Significant incidents

Board reporting occurs quarterly using a cybersecurity dashboard.

The CISO stated that policy reviews occur annually and ownership has been assigned for all enterprise security policies.

---

## Assessor Observations

Positive observations

- Strong executive engagement
- Clearly defined governance structure
- Formal committee charter
- Executive reporting process documented

Improvement opportunities

- Risk acceptance process could be more formalized.
- Executive metrics focus heavily on operational indicators rather than business risk.
- Governance dashboard lacks trend analysis.
