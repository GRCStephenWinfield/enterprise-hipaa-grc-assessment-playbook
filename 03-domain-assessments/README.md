# 03 – Governance Domain Assessment

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance Assessment Portfolio**

This section contains the Governance domain assessment for Magnolia Regional Medical Center.

The Governance domain evaluates executive oversight, policy management, strategic alignment, accountability, risk governance, committee operations, regulatory compliance, security investment, Board reporting, and continuous improvement.

---

## Domain Purpose

The Governance assessment is designed to determine whether cybersecurity activities are:

- Directed by executive leadership
- Aligned with organizational objectives
- Supported by approved policies and standards
- Assigned to accountable owners
- Integrated with enterprise risk management
- Measured through meaningful metrics
- Reported to executive leadership and the Board
- Continuously reviewed and improved
