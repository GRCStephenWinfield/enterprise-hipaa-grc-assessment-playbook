# 19 – AI Governance & Emerging Technology

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The AI Governance & Emerging Technology (AIGET) domain evaluates the organization's ability to govern Artificial Intelligence (AI), Generative AI, Machine Learning (ML), and emerging technologies in a secure, ethical, transparent, and compliant manner.

This assessment examines AI governance structures, AI inventory management, risk classification, model validation, privacy, fairness, explainability, AI security, Generative AI controls, third-party AI governance, MLOps, regulatory compliance, workforce readiness, and continuous improvement.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to determine whether Artificial Intelligence technologies are governed appropriately throughout their lifecycle while supporting business innovation, regulatory compliance, cybersecurity, privacy, and ethical AI principles.

The assessment evaluates:

- AI Governance
- AI Accountability
- AI Inventory
- AI Classification
- AI Lifecycle Management
- AI Risk Assessments
- Model Validation
- Independent Review
- Data Governance
- Privacy
- Bias & Fairness
- Explainability
- Human Oversight
- AI Security
- Adversarial AI
- Generative AI Governance
- Prompt Security
- Third-Party AI Governance
- Foundation Models
- Clinical AI Safety
- MLOps
- AI Incident Response
- AI Regulatory Compliance
- Workforce AI Governance
- Continuous Improvement
