# Purpose

The Milestone Tracking Register provides a centralized mechanism for planning, monitoring, reporting, and controlling all major milestones associated with the Enterprise HIPAA Cybersecurity Assessment.

The register enables the assessment team and client leadership to monitor progress, identify schedule risks, manage dependencies, support executive reporting, and ensure timely completion of all engagement deliverables.

# Dependency Management

Dependencies shall identify work that must be completed before another milestone can begin.

Examples include:

- Interview completion before findings validation
- Evidence collection before control testing
- QA review before executive reporting
- Risk scoring before POA&M development
- Findings approval before executive presentation

---

# Schedule Variance

Track schedule performance using:

```text
Schedule Variance =
Actual Finish Date − Planned Finish Date
```

Positive variance indicates a delay.

Negative variance indicates early completion.

# Escalation Criteria

Escalate when:

- A critical-path milestone is delayed.
- A milestone exceeds five business days past due.
- Multiple dependencies are blocked.
- Executive approval is overdue.
- Schedule variance exceeds agreed thresholds.
- Regulatory deadlines are at risk.

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Review milestone status before every client meeting.
- Focus attention on critical-path milestones.
- Escalate schedule risks early.
- Keep milestone descriptions measurable.
- Update progress continuously rather than at milestone completion.

## Common Pitfalls

- Marking milestones complete before QA review.
- Ignoring dependencies.
- Failing to update actual completion dates.
- Delaying escalation of schedule issues.
- Treating milestone tracking as a one-time planning exercise.

## Red Flags

- Multiple critical-path milestones delayed.
- Repeated milestone slippage.
- Executive approvals pending for extended periods.
- Deliverables marked complete without evidence.
- Milestone owners unable to provide status updates.
