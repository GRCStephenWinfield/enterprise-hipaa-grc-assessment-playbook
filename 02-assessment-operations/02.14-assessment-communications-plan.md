# Purpose

This Communications Plan establishes the communication strategy, governance, reporting structure, meeting cadence, stakeholder notifications, escalation procedures, and documentation standards for the Enterprise HIPAA Cybersecurity Assessment.

Effective communication is essential to ensure assessment activities remain coordinated, transparent, timely, and aligned with executive expectations while minimizing disruption to patient care and business operations.

This plan defines:

- Who receives information
- What information is communicated
- When communications occur
- How communications are delivered
- Who is responsible for each communication
- Required approvals
- Documentation requirements

# Consultant Notes

## Best Practices

- Communicate early and often.
- Tailor communications to the audience.
- Keep executive updates concise.
- Focus on business impact rather than technical detail.
- Track every action item until closure.
- Document all major decisions.

## Common Pitfalls

- Waiting until weekly meetings to communicate critical issues.
- Sending overly technical updates to executives.
- Failing to assign owners to action items.
- Poor meeting documentation.
- Inconsistent messaging across stakeholder groups.
- Missing follow-up on agreed actions.

## Red Flags

- Stakeholders report conflicting information.
- Executive leadership is surprised by assessment findings.
- Action items remain open without owners.
- Repeated missed meetings.
- Status reports become inconsistent.
- Critical issues are communicated too late.
