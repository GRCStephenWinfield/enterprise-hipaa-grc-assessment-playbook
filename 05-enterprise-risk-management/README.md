# 05 – Enterprise Risk Management

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) domain evaluates the organization's ability to identify, assess, prioritize, treat, monitor, and communicate cybersecurity risk across the enterprise.

This assessment examines governance, risk methodology, risk appetite, executive reporting, third-party risk integration, and continuous improvement to determine the maturity of the organization's cybersecurity risk management program.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to determine whether enterprise cybersecurity risks are consistently identified, evaluated, managed, communicated, and monitored in accordance with business objectives and regulatory requirements.

The assessment evaluates:

- Enterprise Cyber Risk Governance
- Risk Assessment Methodology
- Risk Register Management
- Threat Identification
- Vulnerability Risk Analysis
- Business Impact Integration
- Risk Scoring
- Risk Treatment
- Risk Monitoring
- Emerging Risk Management
- Third-Party Risk
- Risk Appetite & Tolerance
- Executive Reporting
- Board Oversight
- Continuous Improvement
