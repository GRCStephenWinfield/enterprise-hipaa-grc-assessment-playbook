# Purpose

This Evidence Register establishes the official inventory of evidence required to perform the Enterprise Risk Management assessment.

The register provides:

- Unique evidence identifiers
- Evidence descriptions
- Source owners
- Evidence classification
- Collection status
- Related controls
- Workpaper references
- Validation status

This document serves as the authoritative evidence index for the Enterprise Risk Management domain.

---

# Evidence Collection Objectives

Evidence collected during this assessment will be used to:

- Validate control design
- Verify operational effectiveness
- Support testing conclusions
- Corroborate interview responses
- Calculate maturity
- Support findings
- Demonstrate regulatory compliance
- Maintain audit defensibility
