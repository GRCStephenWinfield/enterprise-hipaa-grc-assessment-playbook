# Purpose

This Plan of Action & Milestones (POA&M) documents the corrective actions required to remediate findings identified during the Enterprise Risk Management assessment.

The POA&M serves as management's official remediation tracking document and establishes accountability for implementing corrective actions, reducing cybersecurity risk, and improving Enterprise Risk Management maturity.

---

# POA&M Status Definitions

| Status | Definition |
|---------|------------|
| Not Started | Corrective action has not begun. |
| In Progress | Remediation activities are underway. |
| Pending Validation | Remediation is complete and awaiting verification. |
| Completed | Corrective action validated and officially closed. |
| Risk Accepted | Executive management has formally accepted the remaining risk. |

# Milestone Tracking Requirements

Each corrective action should include the following milestones:

- Planning completed
- Resources assigned
- Implementation initiated
- Midpoint review completed
- Corrective action implemented
- Validation completed
- Executive approval obtained (where applicable)
- Official closure documented

---

# Validation Requirements

Corrective actions should be validated using one or more of the following methods:

- Document review
- Evidence verification
- Independent testing
- Management approval
- Executive approval
- Board approval
- Internal Audit verification
- Follow-up assessment

---

# Management Reporting

Management should review POA&M progress:

- Monthly for High-priority corrective actions
- Quarterly for Medium-priority corrective actions
- Semi-annually for Low-priority corrective actions

Reports should include:

- Overall completion percentage
- Overdue corrective actions
- Upcoming milestones
- High-risk remediation status
- Validation results
- Risks requiring executive escalation
