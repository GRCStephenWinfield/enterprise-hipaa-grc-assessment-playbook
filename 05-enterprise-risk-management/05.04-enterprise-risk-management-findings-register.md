# Purpose

This Findings Register consolidates all observations identified during the Enterprise Risk Management assessment into a single authoritative tracking document.

Each finding represents a condition identified through evidence review, interviews, technical testing, or control validation. Findings support management decision-making and provide the basis for recommendations, risk treatment, and corrective action planning.

---

# Finding Severity Definitions

| Severity | Definition |
|----------|------------|
| Critical | Immediate threat to patient safety, regulatory compliance, or organizational operations requiring urgent remediation. |
| High | Significant weakness requiring timely management attention. |
| Moderate | Control weakness that increases organizational risk but is not immediately critical. |
| Low | Minor control deficiency or opportunity for improvement. |
| Informational | Observation that does not represent a control deficiency but may improve program maturity. |
