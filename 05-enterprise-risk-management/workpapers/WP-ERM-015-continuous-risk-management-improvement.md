# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a structured process for continuously improving its Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) program.

The assessment determines whether the organization measures program effectiveness, incorporates lessons learned, monitors regulatory changes, updates governance processes, and continually enhances cybersecurity risk management capabilities.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Performs periodic ERM program reviews.
- Measures ERM effectiveness.
- Tracks corrective actions.
- Incorporates lessons learned.
- Updates methodologies based on organizational changes.
- Monitors regulatory developments.
- Measures program maturity.
- Implements continuous improvement initiatives.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to continually improve Enterprise Risk Management may result in:

- Repeated audit findings.
- Stagnant cybersecurity maturity.
- Outdated risk methodologies.
- Regulatory noncompliance.
- Failure to respond to changing threats.
- Inefficient governance processes.
- Missed improvement opportunities.
- Reduced organizational resilience.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Formal improvement process
- Regular program reviews
- Lessons learned incorporated
- Maturity assessments performed
- Regulatory monitoring active
- Improvement initiatives completed

### Partially Effective

- Improvement activities occur
- Limited documentation
- Inconsistent follow-up
- Improvement metrics incomplete

### Ineffective

- No formal improvement process
- No maturity assessments
- Lessons learned not incorporated
- No corrective action tracking
- Governance remains static
