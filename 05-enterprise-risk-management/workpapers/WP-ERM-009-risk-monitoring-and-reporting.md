# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains an effective process for continuously monitoring cybersecurity risks and communicating meaningful, timely, and actionable risk information to executive leadership and the Board of Directors.

The assessment determines whether changes in the organization's risk posture are identified, evaluated, communicated, and acted upon throughout the cybersecurity risk lifecycle.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Continuously monitors cybersecurity risks.
- Reviews risk status on a defined schedule.
- Tracks changes in risk levels.
- Uses Key Risk Indicators (KRIs).
- Escalates significant risks appropriately.
- Reports meaningful risk information to executive leadership.
- Provides strategic cybersecurity reporting to the Board.
- Uses risk reporting to support decision-making.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to continuously monitor and report cybersecurity risks may result in:

- High risks remaining unnoticed.
- Delayed executive decision-making.
- Untreated emerging threats.
- Regulatory deficiencies.
- Repeated security incidents.
- Inaccurate enterprise risk posture.
- Ineffective Board oversight.
- Poor cybersecurity investment decisions.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Continuous monitoring process
- Meaningful KRIs
- Executive reporting
- Board reporting
- Documented escalation
- Strategic decision support

### Partially Effective

- Monitoring exists
- Limited trend analysis
- Inconsistent reporting
- Minor escalation weaknesses

### Ineffective

- No formal monitoring
- No KRIs
- Minimal executive reporting
- No Board visibility
- Undefined escalation
