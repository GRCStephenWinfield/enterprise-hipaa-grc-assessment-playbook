# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a formal process for identifying, assessing, monitoring, and responding to emerging cybersecurity risks that could affect clinical operations, patient safety, regulatory compliance, and organizational resilience.

The assessment determines whether new technologies, evolving threat actors, regulatory changes, and healthcare industry trends are incorporated into enterprise risk management before material business impacts occur.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Identifies emerging cybersecurity risks.
- Monitors changes in the threat landscape.
- Evaluates technology-related risks.
- Assesses healthcare-specific emerging threats.
- Updates enterprise risks accordingly.
- Communicates emerging risks to executive leadership.
- Incorporates emerging risks into strategic planning.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to identify emerging cybersecurity risks may result in:

- Delayed response to new attack techniques.
- Inadequate protection of patient-care systems.
- Increased ransomware exposure.
- Regulatory noncompliance.
- Strategic investment gaps.
- Business disruption.
- Technology adoption without appropriate security controls.
- Increased organizational risk.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Formal emerging-risk program
- Healthcare-focused monitoring
- Strategic planning integration
- Executive communication
- Continuous reassessment
- Risk Register integration

### Partially Effective

- Emerging-risk activities exist
- Limited documentation
- Irregular reporting
- Partial strategic integration

### Ineffective

- No formal process
- Reactive approach
- No executive awareness
- No strategic planning integration
