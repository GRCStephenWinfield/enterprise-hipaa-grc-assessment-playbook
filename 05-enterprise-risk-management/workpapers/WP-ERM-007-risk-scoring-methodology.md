# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center uses a documented, repeatable, and consistently applied methodology for scoring cybersecurity risks.

The assessment determines whether likelihood, impact, inherent risk, residual risk, and treatment priorities are calculated using standardized criteria that support executive decision-making and regulatory compliance.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Uses an approved risk scoring methodology.
- Applies consistent likelihood criteria.
- Applies consistent impact criteria.
- Separately evaluates inherent and residual risk.
- Considers business and clinical impacts.
- Includes patient safety in risk scoring.
- Uses standardized risk thresholds.
- Produces repeatable and defensible results.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to maintain a standardized risk scoring methodology may result in:

- Inconsistent risk ratings
- Improper prioritization
- Misallocation of cybersecurity resources
- Executive decisions based on inaccurate information
- Regulatory deficiencies
- Untreated High and Critical risks
- Difficulty demonstrating due diligence during audits

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Approved methodology
- Consistent scoring
- Repeatable calculations
- Patient safety integrated
- Business context considered
- Executive-approved thresholds

### Partially Effective

- Methodology documented
- Minor inconsistencies
- Limited business context
- Occasional scoring differences

### Ineffective

- No formal methodology
- Subjective scoring
- Undefined thresholds
- Inconsistent implementation
- No separation of inherent and residual risk
