# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center has established, documented, approved, communicated, and applied cybersecurity risk appetite and risk tolerance statements that guide enterprise decision-making.

The assessment determines whether executive leadership and the Board have clearly defined acceptable levels of cybersecurity risk and whether operational decisions align with those established thresholds.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Maintains documented cybersecurity risk appetite statements.
- Defines measurable cybersecurity risk tolerance thresholds.
- Obtains Board approval.
- Communicates risk appetite across the organization.
- Uses risk appetite during treatment decisions.
- Uses risk tolerance when approving exceptions.
- Reviews appetite periodically.
- Updates appetite as organizational conditions change.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to define cybersecurity risk appetite may result in:

- Inconsistent risk decisions.
- Conflicting executive priorities.
- Excessive risk acceptance.
- Underinvestment in cybersecurity.
- Ineffective governance.
- Regulatory deficiencies.
- Poor strategic alignment.
- Uncontrolled residual risk.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Documented and approved appetite.
- Measurable tolerance thresholds.
- Consistent operational application.
- Executive and Board oversight.
- Periodic review.
- Enterprise-wide communication.

### Partially Effective

- Appetite documented.
- Minor communication gaps.
- Limited operational application.
- Inconsistent review cadence.

### Ineffective

- No documented appetite.
- Undefined tolerances.
- No Board approval.
- No operational use.
- No periodic review.
