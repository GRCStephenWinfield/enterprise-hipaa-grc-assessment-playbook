# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a formally documented and operational Enterprise Cyber Risk Management Program that establishes governance, accountability, methodology, oversight, and continuous management of cybersecurity risk across the organization.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization has implemented an enterprise-wide cybersecurity risk management program that:

- Is formally approved
- Is consistently implemented
- Is integrated into Enterprise Risk Management (ERM)
- Supports HIPAA Security Rule compliance
- Aligns with organizational strategy
- Supports executive and Board decision-making

# Risks Addressed

Failure to maintain a formal enterprise cyber risk management program may result in:

- Inconsistent risk decisions
- Undocumented risk ownership
- Regulatory noncompliance
- Poor investment prioritization
- Increased patient-safety exposure
- Executive decision-making based on incomplete information
- Inability to demonstrate due diligence

# Evaluation Criteria

The control is considered:

### Effective

- Program formally documented
- Executive approved
- Organization-wide implementation
- Annual review performed
- Risk governance functioning

### Partially Effective

- Program exists but documentation incomplete
- Reviews inconsistent
- Limited executive engagement
- Governance gaps identified

### Ineffective

- No formal program
- No ownership
- No executive approval
- No evidence of implementation
