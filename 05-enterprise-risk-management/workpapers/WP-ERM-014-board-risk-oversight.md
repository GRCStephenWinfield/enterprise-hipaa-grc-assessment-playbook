# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center's Board of Directors provides effective governance and oversight of enterprise cybersecurity risk.

The assessment determines whether the Board receives sufficient information to oversee cybersecurity strategy, risk appetite, resilience, regulatory compliance, and organizational preparedness while fulfilling its governance responsibilities.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the Board:

- Receives regular cybersecurity briefings.
- Reviews enterprise cyber risk.
- Understands organizational risk posture.
- Reviews significant cybersecurity incidents.
- Oversees cybersecurity strategy.
- Approves cybersecurity risk appetite.
- Monitors major remediation initiatives.
- Supports cybersecurity investment decisions.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to provide effective Board oversight may result in:

- Weak cybersecurity governance.
- Poor strategic direction.
- Inadequate funding.
- Delayed executive accountability.
- Regulatory scrutiny.
- Increased organizational exposure.
- Failure to manage enterprise cyber risk.
- Reduced organizational resilience.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Board receives regular cybersecurity reporting.
- Strategic oversight demonstrated.
- Risk appetite approved.
- Board education documented.
- Significant risks reviewed.
- Governance responsibilities fulfilled.

### Partially Effective

- Board oversight exists.
- Reporting inconsistent.
- Limited strategic discussion.
- Board education incomplete.

### Ineffective

- No regular Board reporting.
- No documented oversight.
- Risk appetite not approved.
- Board unaware of enterprise cyber risk.
- Governance responsibilities undefined.
