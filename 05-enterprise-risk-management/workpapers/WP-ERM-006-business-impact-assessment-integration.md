# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center integrates Business Impact Assessment (BIA) results into enterprise cybersecurity risk management.

The assessment determines whether cybersecurity risks are evaluated based on operational, clinical, financial, regulatory, and patient-safety impacts rather than solely on technical considerations.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Maintains a formal Business Impact Assessment process.
- Identifies critical business functions.
- Identifies critical clinical functions.
- Evaluates patient-safety impacts.
- Uses Recovery Time Objectives (RTOs) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPOs) during cyber risk analysis.
- Integrates BIA results into enterprise risk assessments.
- Uses BIA results to prioritize remediation activities.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to integrate Business Impact Analysis into cybersecurity risk management may result in:

- Patient-care disruption.
- Improper prioritization of cybersecurity investments.
- Extended EHR outages.
- Delayed recovery of critical systems.
- Revenue-cycle interruption.
- Regulatory deficiencies.
- Increased organizational downtime.
- Inaccurate enterprise risk ratings.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Formal BIA methodology
- Critical functions identified
- Patient safety integrated
- Recovery objectives documented
- Enterprise risk integration demonstrated
- Executive approval obtained

### Partially Effective

- BIA exists
- Minor documentation gaps
- Limited enterprise integration
- Recovery objectives inconsistently maintained

### Ineffective

- No formal BIA
- Critical processes unidentified
- Patient safety omitted
- Recovery objectives undefined
- No integration with enterprise risk
