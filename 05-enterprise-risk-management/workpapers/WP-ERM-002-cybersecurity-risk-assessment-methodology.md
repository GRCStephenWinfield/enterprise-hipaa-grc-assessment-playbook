# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a documented, repeatable, and consistently applied cybersecurity risk assessment methodology that aligns with recognized industry standards and regulatory requirements.

The methodology should support consistent risk identification, analysis, prioritization, treatment, communication, and monitoring across the enterprise.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization has implemented a formal cybersecurity risk assessment methodology that:

- Is documented and approved
- Defines risk terminology
- Uses consistent scoring criteria
- Evaluates inherent and residual risk
- Includes patient safety and business impact
- Supports HIPAA Security Rule compliance
- Aligns with enterprise risk management
- Produces repeatable results

# Risks Addressed

Failure to maintain a standardized methodology may result in:

- Inconsistent risk ratings
- Improper prioritization
- Untreated High risks
- Regulatory deficiencies
- Poor investment decisions
- Inaccurate executive reporting
- Reduced audit defensibility

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Documented methodology
- Consistent scoring
- Regulatory alignment
- Enterprise-wide use
- Annual review
- Repeatable results

### Partially Effective

- Methodology exists
- Minor inconsistencies
- Limited documentation
- Occasional scoring differences

### Ineffective

- No formal methodology
- Subjective scoring
- No approval
- No repeatability
- Inconsistent implementation
