# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a formal, repeatable, and well-governed process for selecting, documenting, implementing, monitoring, and validating cybersecurity risk treatment activities.

The assessment determines whether identified risks are addressed through appropriate treatment decisions that align with the organization's risk appetite, regulatory obligations, and business objectives.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the organization:

- Documents risk treatment decisions.
- Selects appropriate treatment strategies.
- Assigns accountable owners.
- Defines implementation milestones.
- Allocates appropriate resources.
- Monitors treatment progress.
- Validates completion of remediation.
- Updates residual risk after treatment.

# Risks Addressed

Failure to effectively manage risk treatment may result in:

- High risks remaining unresolved.
- Regulatory noncompliance.
- Repeated audit findings.
- Ineffective remediation.
- Extended exposure to cyber threats.
- Inaccurate residual-risk ratings.
- Delayed executive awareness.
- Poor accountability.

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Treatment plans documented
- Ownership assigned
- Progress monitored
- Residual risk reassessed
- Completion validated
- Executive oversight demonstrated

### Partially Effective

- Treatment process exists
- Minor documentation gaps
- Limited monitoring
- Occasional validation deficiencies

### Ineffective

- No formal treatment process
- Undefined ownership
- No monitoring
- No validation
- Residual risk not updated
