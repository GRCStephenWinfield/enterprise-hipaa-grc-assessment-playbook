# Purpose

This workpaper evaluates whether Magnolia Regional Medical Center maintains a comprehensive Enterprise Cyber Risk Register that accurately documents, prioritizes, tracks, monitors, and reports cybersecurity risks throughout their lifecycle.

The Enterprise Risk Register should serve as the organization's authoritative repository for cybersecurity risk information and support executive decision-making, regulatory compliance, and continuous risk management.

---

# Control Objective

Determine whether the Enterprise Cyber Risk Register:

- Is centrally maintained
- Contains complete and current risk information
- Uses standardized risk statements
- Assigns accountable risk owners
- Includes inherent and residual risk ratings
- Documents treatment decisions
- Tracks remediation progress
- Supports executive reporting
- Is periodically reviewed and updated

# Risks Addressed

An ineffective Enterprise Risk Register may result in:

- Unidentified cybersecurity risks
- Duplicate or inconsistent risk records
- Untreated High-risk conditions
- Unclear accountability
- Poor executive visibility
- Ineffective resource prioritization
- Regulatory noncompliance
- Failure to monitor risk over time

# Evaluation Criteria

### Effective

- Complete register
- Current information
- Assigned owners
- Consistent reviews
- Accurate reporting
- Executive visibility

### Partially Effective

- Minor documentation gaps
- Inconsistent review frequency
- Occasional ownership issues
- Minor reporting weaknesses

### Ineffective

- Incomplete register
- Missing ownership
- Outdated information
- No review process
- Poor executive reporting
