# Control Details

## CTRL-ERM-001 — Enterprise Cyber Risk Management Program

### Objective

Determine whether the organization maintains a formally documented enterprise cybersecurity risk management program approved by executive leadership.

### Expected Evidence

- Enterprise Risk Management Policy
- Cybersecurity Risk Management Standard
- Executive approval records
- Governance Committee minutes

### Testing Procedures

- Review policies.
- Verify executive approval.
- Confirm annual review.
- Interview Enterprise Risk Manager.

---

## CTRL-ERM-002 — Cybersecurity Risk Assessment Methodology

### Objective

Determine whether a standardized methodology exists for identifying, analyzing, scoring, documenting, and prioritizing cybersecurity risks.

### Expected Evidence

- Risk assessment methodology
- Risk scoring guide
- Risk calculation examples
- Risk assessment templates

### Testing Procedures

- Review methodology.
- Verify consistent application.
- Validate scoring samples.
- Compare methodology against NIST SP 800-30.

---

## CTRL-ERM-003 — Enterprise Risk Register Management

### Objective

Determine whether enterprise cyber risks are documented, assigned, tracked, and periodically reviewed.

### Expected Evidence

- Enterprise Risk Register
- Risk ownership records
- Review history
- Closed-risk documentation

### Testing Procedures

- Review risk register.
- Verify ownership.
- Sample open and closed risks.
- Confirm periodic review.

---

## CTRL-ERM-004 — Threat Identification Process

### Objective

Determine whether the organization systematically identifies relevant cyber threat sources affecting clinical and business operations.

### Expected Evidence

- Threat intelligence reports
- Threat modeling documentation
- Industry alerts
- Internal threat assessments

---

## CTRL-ERM-005 — Vulnerability Risk Analysis

### Objective

Determine whether identified vulnerabilities are incorporated into enterprise risk assessments.

### Expected Evidence

- Vulnerability assessments
- Penetration tests
- Vulnerability management reports
- Risk register references

---

## CTRL-ERM-006 — Business Impact Assessment Integration

### Objective

Determine whether business and patient-safety impacts are considered during cybersecurity risk analysis.

### Expected Evidence

- Business Impact Analysis (BIA)
- Disaster Recovery documentation
- Clinical impact assessments
- Patient-safety evaluations

---

## CTRL-ERM-007 — Risk Scoring Methodology

### Objective

Determine whether inherent and residual risks are calculated using an approved and consistently applied methodology.

### Expected Evidence

- Risk scoring matrix
- Likelihood criteria
- Impact criteria
- Sample calculations

---

## CTRL-ERM-008 — Risk Treatment Process

### Objective

Determine whether documented risk-treatment decisions are approved, assigned, tracked, and completed.

### Expected Evidence

- Risk treatment plans
- POA&M records
- Management approvals
- Closure documentation

---

## CTRL-ERM-009 — Risk Monitoring and Reporting

### Objective

Determine whether cybersecurity risks are periodically monitored and communicated to executive leadership.

### Expected Evidence

- Executive dashboards
- Risk committee reports
- Monthly risk summaries
- Trending reports

---

## CTRL-ERM-010 — Emerging Risk Management

### Objective

Determine whether the organization identifies and evaluates emerging cybersecurity threats and technologies.

### Expected Evidence

- Emerging-risk register
- Threat intelligence briefings
- AI risk assessments
- Cloud risk reviews

---

## CTRL-ERM-011 — Third-Party Cyber Risk Integration

### Objective

Determine whether third-party cybersecurity risks are incorporated into enterprise risk management.

### Expected Evidence

- Vendor risk assessments
- Business Associate Agreements
- Third-party risk register
- Critical supplier inventory

---

## CTRL-ERM-012 — Risk Appetite and Tolerance

### Objective

Determine whether executive leadership has defined cybersecurity risk appetite and tolerance statements.

### Expected Evidence

- Risk appetite statement
- Risk tolerance thresholds
- Board approvals
- Executive communications

---

## CTRL-ERM-013 — Executive Risk Reporting

### Objective

Determine whether executives receive timely and meaningful cybersecurity risk information.

### Expected Evidence

- Executive dashboards
- Quarterly reports
- KRIs
- Executive meeting minutes

---

## CTRL-ERM-014 — Board Risk Oversight

### Objective

Determine whether the Board receives sufficient cybersecurity risk information to fulfill governance responsibilities.

### Expected Evidence

- Board presentations
- Committee minutes
- Board dashboards
- Annual cyber briefings

---

## CTRL-ERM-015 — Continuous Risk Improvement

### Objective

Determine whether the enterprise risk management program measures maturity and continuously improves over time.

### Expected Evidence

- Maturity assessments
- Lessons learned
- Continuous improvement plans
- Annual program reviews
