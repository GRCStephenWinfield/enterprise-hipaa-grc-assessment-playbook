# Purpose

This Enterprise Risk Register documents the cybersecurity risks identified during the Enterprise Risk Management assessment.

Each risk includes a standardized risk statement, likelihood, impact, inherent risk, residual risk, treatment strategy, ownership, review cadence, and current status.

This register supports executive decision-making, ongoing governance, regulatory compliance, and continuous risk monitoring.

# Treatment Strategy Definitions

| Strategy | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Mitigate | Implement controls to reduce likelihood and/or impact. |
| Accept | Formally acknowledge and retain the risk. |
| Transfer | Shift the risk through insurance, contracts, or outsourcing. |
| Avoid | Eliminate the activity creating the risk. |

---

# Risk Review Requirements

Each enterprise risk shall include:

- Assigned business owner
- Review frequency
- Residual risk assessment
- Current treatment status
- Supporting evidence
- Executive approval (where required)
- Target completion date
- Validation after remediation

---

# Risk Ownership Responsibilities

Risk owners are responsible for:

- Monitoring assigned risks.
- Implementing treatment plans.
- Reporting progress.
- Updating risk status.
- Escalating significant changes.
- Validating remediation effectiveness.
