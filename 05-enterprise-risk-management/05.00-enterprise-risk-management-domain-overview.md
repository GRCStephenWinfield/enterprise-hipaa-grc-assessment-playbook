# Purpose

The Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) domain evaluates Magnolia Regional Medical Center's ability to identify, assess, prioritize, treat, monitor, and communicate cybersecurity risks affecting organizational objectives, patient safety, regulatory compliance, financial stability, and operational resilience.

This assessment determines whether cybersecurity risk management is consistently integrated into enterprise governance and business decision-making.

---

# Domain Objectives

The Enterprise Risk Management assessment will determine whether the organization:

- Maintains a formal cybersecurity risk management program.
- Performs periodic enterprise cybersecurity risk assessments.
- Identifies internal and external cyber threats.
- Identifies vulnerabilities affecting information assets.
- Evaluates business impact and likelihood.
- Calculates inherent and residual risk.
- Defines organizational risk appetite and tolerance.
- Selects appropriate risk-treatment strategies.
- Monitors risk throughout its lifecycle.
- Communicates risk to executive leadership and the Board.
- Integrates cyber risk into enterprise risk management (ERM).
- Supports HIPAA Security Rule risk-analysis requirements.

# Risk Statement

Failure to maintain an effective enterprise cybersecurity risk management program may result in unidentified threats, untreated vulnerabilities, ineffective resource allocation, increased regulatory exposure, patient-safety impacts, financial loss, and reduced organizational resilience.
