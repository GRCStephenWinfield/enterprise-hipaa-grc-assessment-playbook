# Purpose

This document summarizes the interviews performed during the Enterprise Risk Management assessment.

Interview objectives are to:

- Validate documented processes
- Confirm operational effectiveness
- Corroborate evidence
- Identify undocumented practices
- Evaluate management awareness
- Assess governance maturity
- Support assessment conclusions

Interview results will be used together with documentary evidence and technical testing.

# Interview Objectives

The interviews are intended to determine whether:

- Risk management processes are consistently followed.
- Roles and responsibilities are understood.
- Risk decisions are appropriately documented.
- Executive oversight is effective.
- Risk reporting supports decision-making.
- Patient safety is incorporated into cyber risk analysis.
- Third-party risks are managed.
- Emerging risks are monitored.
- Risk acceptance follows organizational policy.

---

# Standard Interview Questions

## Enterprise Risk Manager

### Objectives

- Understand enterprise risk methodology.
- Validate risk governance.
- Review risk lifecycle.

### Questions

1. Describe the organization's cybersecurity risk-management methodology.
2. How frequently is the enterprise cyber risk assessment updated?
3. How are new cyber risks identified?
4. How is residual risk determined?
5. How are risk owners assigned?
6. How are treatment decisions tracked?
7. What triggers reassessment?
8. How are accepted risks monitored?
9. How is risk information communicated to executives?
10. What improvements are currently planned?

---

# Interview Evaluation Criteria

Interview responses should demonstrate:

- Consistency
- Process maturity
- Executive awareness
- Alignment with documentation
- Regulatory understanding
- Defined accountability
- Risk ownership
- Continuous improvement

---

# Response Validation

Interview responses shall be validated against:

- Enterprise Risk Management Policy
- Risk Register
- Risk Assessment Methodology
- Risk Appetite Statement
- Risk Committee Minutes
- Executive Dashboards
- HIPAA Security Risk Analysis
- Internal Audit Reports

Interview responses alone are not sufficient evidence.

---

# Interview Themes

The assessor should evaluate whether recurring themes emerge regarding:

- Risk ownership
- Risk scoring consistency
- Executive reporting
- Emerging threats
- Third-party risks
- Resource constraints
- Patient safety
- Risk acceptance
- Regulatory compliance

Repeated themes should be evaluated for potential findings.

---

# Potential Red Flags

The following interview responses require additional testing:

- Inconsistent explanations of risk scoring.
- Undefined risk appetite.
- Risks without assigned owners.
- Accepted risks with no expiration.
- Executive reports that omit High risks.
- Infrequent risk reassessment.
- Limited consideration of patient safety.
- Weak third-party oversight.
- Lack of emerging-risk monitoring.
- Inconsistent documentation.
