# 15 – Privacy & Regulatory Compliance

> **Enterprise Cybersecurity Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Assessment Portfolio**

The Privacy & Regulatory Compliance (PRC) domain evaluates the organization's ability to establish, implement, monitor, and continuously improve an enterprise privacy program that complies with applicable laws, regulations, contractual obligations, and industry standards.

This assessment examines privacy governance, regulatory obligations, data inventories, privacy notices, individual rights, data retention, privacy impact assessments, third-party privacy management, cross-border data transfers, breach notification, workforce awareness, regulatory response, and continuous improvement.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this assessment is to determine whether the organization has implemented an effective privacy and regulatory compliance program capable of protecting sensitive information while meeting legal and contractual obligations.

The assessment evaluates:

- Privacy Governance
- Regulatory Compliance
- Data Inventory & Data Flows
- Privacy Notices
- Consent Management
- Minimum Necessary Use
- Individual Privacy Rights
- Data Retention & Disposal
- De-identification
- Privacy by Design
- Privacy Impact Assessments
- Third-Party Privacy
- Business Associate Management
- Cross-Border Data Transfers
- Privacy Incidents
- Breach Notification
- Workforce Privacy Training
- Regulatory Audits
- Continuous Improvement
